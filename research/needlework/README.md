# Charted needlework: a pattern library for the planisphere face

Historical pixel art transcribed cell for cell from public-domain scans: German and
Italian pattern books (Modelbücher) of 1524–1615, the Paris lacis book of 1605, a
German embroiderer's album of c. 1596, a Paris marking book of 1819, DMC charts of
1885–1890, and Dutch, English and American samplers. For filigree in the ring and
corners, seasonal motifs, small creatures and village pieces, stars, and numerals.

- `sources.md`: where every scan came from (institution, accession, image ids) and its licence (public domain / CC0 only).
- `motifs/<slug>.txt`: one transcription per file: a header (source, page, crop box, size, symmetry, colours, use, status, how it was verified) and then the chart.
- `images/<slug>.jpg`: the crop it was read from; `images/<slug>_ver.jpg`: the overlay used to check it (green dot = charted cell confirmed on the scan; red = charted but not on the scan; blue square = on the scan but not charted).
- `images/_charts-*.png`: every chart in the library rendered as pixel art on one sheet (a visual index; regenerated from the motif files).

Chart convention: `#` (or one letter per colour, keyed in the header) = a stitched or
printed cell, `.` = ground. Rows read top to bottom as on the scan. Bands give the
period and at least two repeats. VERIFIED = every cell checked against the scan by an
overlay (the header gives the count); UNVERIFIED = described or read but not checked
cell for cell (the header says why).

## Best for the face

(kept up to date as the library grows; sizes in cells)

- Corner stars: `quentel1529-star-15` (eight-lobed star, 15×15, full symmetry; printed unchanged in 1529 and in 1819), `ch-18616385-star-11` (eight-pointed star with forked arms, 11×11), `quentel1529-lozenge-10` (10×10 sparkle), `bk-1968-11-four-petal-flower` (7×7 quatrefoil).
- The Sun and the Moon: `quentel1544-wheel-15` (an eight-spoked wheel, 15×15: a sun wheel or dial), `dmc1890s1-crescent-moon` (a crescent with a face in profile, 7×14), `album1596-luna-moon-disc` (full moon with a face, 15×14).
- How a band turns a corner: `tryntje-corner-battlement` (frame line, checker and battlement meeting at the corner, 25×18); `vinciolo1588-handkerchief-corner-71` (a lacis handkerchief corner, large).
- Coloured ring bands, painted c. 1596 in verdigris, orange, rose and black: `album1596-flame-band` (4 rows, period 4), `album1596-oval-bead-band` (12 rows, period 8), `album1596-bracket-cross-band` (14 rows, period 10).
- Ring bands, one-cell line work: `tryntje-battlement-band` (5 rows, period 8), `dmc1890-tee-fret-band` (5 rows, period 6), `tryntje-spiral-key-band` (7 rows, period 8), `bk-1968-11-greek-key-band` (9 rows, period 11), `quentel1529-triangle-band` (a 4-row dog-tooth from one tier), `quentel1529-acorn-band` (7 rows, period 6: autumn).
- Seasons: spring `quentel1529-rosette-13`, `ostaus1567-blossom-tree-15`, `quentel1529-lily-11x9`; summer `vosterman1527-leafy-tree-15`; autumn `ch-18616709-grape-bunch` (11×13), `dmc1890-acorn-10`, `bk-1968-11-vine-leaf`; winter `petit1819-snowflake-7` (7×7 ice crystal), `quentel1544-fir-band` (a row of firs, 10 rows). Mignerak's four seasons (1605) are described with crops in `mignerak1605-four-seasons` (line drawings on net, not countable).
- Creatures: `dillmont-alphabete-owl` (owl on a branch, 9×13 + branch), `quentel1529-small-bird-11x9` with `quentel1529-lily-11x9` (a pair of birds facing a lily: a centre piece), `quentel1529-bird-11x15`, `bk-15282-cock`, `bk-1980-63-bird-on-post`.
- Village: `dillmont-alphabete-windmill`, `quentel1529-tree-15`, `bk-1980-63-paling-fence`, `bk-1980-63-gate`, `bk-1980-63-anchor`.
- Numerals: period sets on one baseline: `album1596-numerals` (c. 1596, 6 rows), `petit1819-numerals` (1819, 7 rows), `tryntje-numerals` (c. 1895, 7 rows), `dillmont-alphabete-numerals-7row`; old-style (hanging) figures: `met-57-122-348-numerals` (1799), `dillmont-alphabete-italic-numerals`.
