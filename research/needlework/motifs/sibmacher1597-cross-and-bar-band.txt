size: period 24 cells x 5 rows (half-period 12: the second half is the first turned upside down)
symmetry: the period plus one column (25 wide, as in the repeats) is mirror left-right; the band is a glide (shift 12 + flip top-bottom)
colours: * = four-point-star symbol (one colour throughout), . = ground
use: ring band (5 rows, one colour: a light filigree that reads as little crosses at 1x)
status: VERIFIED
verified: cells between the detected (uneven) etched grid lines, rows on slanted lines (rows measured at four places along the band); the whole band (42 x 5 cells, one and three-quarter periods): ink in each cell (Otsu) 210/210 agree; k-means on the cell patches 210/210. Overlay: images/sibmacher1597-cross-and-bar-band_ver.jpg (left half; green = star cell confirmed)
notes: Pairs of small crosses (.**. / **** / .**.) above four-cell bars, then the same pair hanging below bars, linked by single-cell steps. All symbols are stars, so the band is a plain two-tone chart.
