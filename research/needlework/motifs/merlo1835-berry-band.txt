title: Berry band: clusters of three dark berries (violet rims, white and blue highlights) between curling green fronds (Berlin work, six colours)
section: Narrow bands
source: Emma Merlo, Neueste Portefeuille für Freundinnen weiblicher Kunst-Arbeiten: eine Auswahl von Guirlanden, allerlei Blumen, Kränzen, Bouquets, Fruchtkörben, Schmetterlingen, Kanten, Arabesken u.s.w. zum künstlichen Stricken, Häkeln, zur Tapisserie- und Mosaik-Arbeit, Pirna/Leipzig: Carl August Friese, c. 1835 (catalogued [183-?]); hand-coloured printed charts ("bei Friese in Leipzig"); Getty Research Institute copy, archive.org neuesteportefeui00merl; public domain (published in the 1830s; no rights statement on the item)

size: period 20 cells x 17 rows (one berry: 4 x 5)
symmetry: the fronds and stalks are mirror top-bottom; the berries are not (every berry has its white and blue highlights at the lower left, as if lit from one side), so the chart has no exact symmetry (sym.py: none); translation only
colours: v = violet (painted RGB about 100,87,104, the rims; light and dark washes of the same paint), k = black (about 5,5,8; some with a faint blue cast), b = blue (about 70,125,160, one reflection cell per berry), l = light green (about 158,173,98; washes from about 110 to 170 in brightness with no pattern between repeats: one green), g = dark green (about 15,24,16), . = paper (about 246,247,227; a white highlight cell inside each berry is left as paper). Face palette: v violet, k ink, b window blue, l light green, g dark green
use: season (autumn: the berry alone, 4 x 5, or the three-berry cluster) / ring band (17 rows: a wide band; the fronds alone make a 7-row leaf band)
status: VERIFIED
verified: grid-paper reader (g4lib cell centres), median colour per cell classified to eight references (paper, a grey class for stains, light green and a dull light green merged into it, blue, violet, dark green, black), very dark cells sorted by hue (green excess with G >= B -> dark green, else black); the period from the majority of the three full units, tiled over the drawn band (64 x 17 cells: 3 units and the 4th unit's single left berry, which closes the band) matches 1080/1088 cells. The 8 others: 4 frond tips at the band's start (they belong to a frond of a unit before the first, which is not drawn); 1 extra dark green cell at a frond tip (unit 2); 1 faint grey stain (163,162,157); 1 berry rim cell painted olive (134,138,106) in unit 1; 1 light green leaf cell painted dark green in unit 3. Overlay (left half above, right half below): images/merlo1835-berry-band_ver.jpg
notes: Each unit: three berries in a triangle (one at the left on the band's axis, one above and one below to the right), joined by light green stalks to a short dark green stem, then a pair of mirror-image fronds curling up and down (dark green midribs, light green leaflets). The berries are drawn like little lit spheres: violet rim, black body, a white cell and a blue cell for the highlight. The berry alone reads well at 1x as a dark fruit; the cluster suits an autumn spot, and the fronds a wreath. A cell beyond the drawn band (a 1-cell leaf stub after the last berry) is not charted.
