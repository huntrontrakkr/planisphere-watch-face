section: Narrow bands

size: period 5 cells x 6 rows
symmetry: mirror left-right (about the T-cross column and about the square's centre) and top-bottom
colours: * = four-point-star symbol, H = vertically hatched cell, x = diagonal-cross symbol (the 2x2 centre of each framed square), . = ground
use: ring band
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice fitted to the etched grid lines; 4 tiled periods overlaid: 104/120 cells agree on ink, and the 16 others are the thin x symbols (too little ink for the threshold), confirmed by eye on the overlay (images/sibmacher1597-narrow-band-b_ver.jpg)
notes: Each square is a 2x2 centre framed by hatched cells, with the corners left open; T-crosses of stars point in from top and bottom between the squares. Binary version: ###.. / .#.## / #.### / #.### / .#.## / ###..
