notes: Upright small birds (sparrow or finch shape) with a long tail sloping to the ground, a dark wing bar across the back, one red and one dark leg (near and far leg), each standing on a short dark perch. The left bird faces right, the right bird faces left; for an exactly mirrored pair flip one of them. Each bird is 75 cells; a good size for the face at 1x. The 1890 one-colour original of this pair is dmc1890s1-bird-pair (Album I, Pl. 32): the left bird agrees cell for cell except the tail's dark lower edge (4 cells), which this colour plate adds; the right bird is redrawn here.
