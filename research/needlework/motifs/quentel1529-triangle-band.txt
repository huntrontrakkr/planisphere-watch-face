title: Sawtooth band (two staggered rows of triangles)
section: Narrow bands
source: Peter Quentel, Eyn New Kunstlichboich, Cologne 1529; The Met 20.50.2(1-49), Public Domain
short-source: Quentel 1529 (Met DP361401)
page: image DP361401 (printed folio 40), top band of the page (four tiers of triangles, i.e. two vertical periods); crop x360-860 y270-580
url: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/354657
image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/original/DP361401.jpg
size: period 8 cells across x 8 rows (one tier pair); 4 rows for a single tier
symmetry: each triangle mirror left-right; the two tiers are offset by half a period (glide)
colours: # = black cell, . = white cell
use: ring band (sawtooth or "dog-tooth" edge: one tier alone, rows 5-8, is a 4-row edging)
status: VERIFIED
verified: tiled 3 periods x 2 tiers and overlaid on the scan: 383/384 cells agree; the one mismatch is an ink speck inside a white cell (images/quentel1529-triangle-band_ver.jpg)
notes: The single tier (rows 5-8: 1,3,5,7 cells) is the simplest possible dog-tooth edging; the full 8-row pair makes a denser band.
