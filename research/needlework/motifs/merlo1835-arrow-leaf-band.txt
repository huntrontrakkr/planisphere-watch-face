verified: grid-paper reader (g4lib cell centres), median colour per cell classified to paper / light green / black (+ a grey class); period from the majority of 9 repeats (3 disagreements, each in one repeat), tiled over the whole drawn band (90 x 10 cells, 9 repeats) matches 897/900 cells. The 3 others: one extra light green cell, one leaf cell painted thinly (175,181,149), one leaf cell painted dense (75,88,42, read as black). Overlay (left half above, right half below): images/merlo1835-arrow-leaf-band_ver.jpg
notes: Each leaf is a 3 x 3 light green blot with a black L along two sides (its shaded edge): upper leaves shaded below and to the right, lower leaves shaded above and to the right, with plain green leaves between, all on a thin green zigzag. At 1x the black L's dominate and read as a row of hooks or arrowheads over a green ribbon: a good, simple ink-and-green filigree.
