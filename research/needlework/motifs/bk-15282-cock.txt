verified: lattice fitted to the stitches; the ground is unbalanced, so the cells are 14.95 x 17.3 px (two threads each way; checked against the weave and the stitch texture by FFT). Filled/empty, notcream channel, in the 24 x 21 frame around the bird: 494/504 cells agree; the 10 others are all image-inked chart-empty cells with values at the threshold (0.48-0.61): fuzz at the body edge and the slanting toe stitches straddling rows 18-19 (images/bk-15282-cock_ver.jpg). Colours by a hue rule on the cell means (green: G-R >= -9, tan: G-R <= -14): 129/131 filled cells agree; the 2 others (comb corner row 3 col 2, neck row 5 col 7; rows and columns counted from 1) are in between and were called tan by eye at 6x zoom
notes: Tan comb (rows 1-3) over a green head band with a one-cell beak (row 4, col 1); a tan neck widening into a solid body with a straight back; two green legs (cols 8 and 12) ending in two-toed feet. The green tail is a sickle loop (up from the rump at row 12, over the top at rows 8-9, down the right side) with a single hanging feather (col 19, rows 13-16). Reads as a cock at 1x; for a weathercock use one colour. Same sampler: crown, houses with steeples, birds facing a flower, rosettes (not charted yet).
