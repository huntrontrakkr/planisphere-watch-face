title: Slanting flame band (rust, red and yellow staircases over a dark rule)
section: Narrow bands
source: Manuscript album of designs for lace and embroidery, German, c. 1596 (woodcut grid paper, designs inked and painted by hand); The Met 53.566.7(1-69), Public Domain
short-source: German album c.1596 (Met DP356737)
page: image DP356737, the lowest band on the page; crop x1540-2010 y2700-2860 of the full scan (the band starts at x about 1570)
url: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/368249
image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/original/DP356737.jpg
size: period 4 cells x 4 rows (+ 1 empty row + a dark rule = 6 rows)
symmetry: none within the unit (each flame slants down to the right); translation only. Mirror the chart for flames leaning the other way
colours: o = rust (dark orange-red), r = red, y = yellow ochre, d = dark brown-black, . = paper. Under the band the painter added a row of white dots, set on the grid lines rather than in the cells (not charted)
use: ring band (a 4-row warm filigree: flames, or autumn leaves; reads at 1x as a running diagonal ribbon)
status: VERIFIED
verified: grid-paper reader for the cell centres, median colour per cell classified to six reference colours; 5 tiled periods over the drawn band: 79/80 cells agree (the one other cell is painted between rust and red); the rule below reads dark brown in all 23 cells. Overlay: images/album1596-flame-band_ver.jpg
notes: Each unit is a staircase of three colours: a rust pair at the top, then yellow-red-rust-rust, then a yellow-red-red step, and a yellow cell between two dark cells at the foot, so a yellow highlight runs down each flame's left edge. The narrowest coloured band in the library.
