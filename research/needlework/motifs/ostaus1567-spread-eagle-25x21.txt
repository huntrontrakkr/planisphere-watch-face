title: Spread eagle (heraldic, wings of stepped feather bars, legs and tail as a dotted lattice)
section: Creatures
source: Giovanni Ostaus, La Vera Perfettione del Disegno di varie sorti di recami, Venice 1567; The Met 21.15.3(1-79), Public Domain
short-source: Ostaus 1567 fol. LXXVII (Met DP362025)
page: image DP362025 (folio LXXVII), upper band, the centre piece between the two lions; x1154-1596 y412-794 of the full scan
url: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/354909
image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/original/DP362025.jpg
size: 25 x 21 cells
symmetry: none exactly: the head (rows 1-5) faces left, the wings, body, legs and tail are mirror left-right about column 13
colours: # = filled cell (cut as a grey-hatched cell in the woodcut), . = white cell
use: creature (an eagle; Aquila, or a crest for the top of the dial); larger than the face's usual pieces
status: VERIFIED
verified: two reads on the white-cell lattice: blob reader 516/525, darkness at each site (Otsu) 517/525. The differences: 6 single cells in the outer columns (col 1 and col 25, rows 5, 11, 17), which are the band's separating dots, not part of the eagle; 2 body cells (row 10, cols 12-13) whose hatching is lighter (darkness just under the threshold; a 6x zoom shows them hatched, as charted); one stray cell for the blob reader. Looked at the overlay: images/ostaus1567-spread-eagle-25x21_ver.jpg
notes: Wings of four horizontal feather bars on each side (the top bar 7 cells, hooked down at the tip), a solid breast, then legs and a tail drawn as an airy lattice of dots and short diagonals. Reads at 1x as a displayed eagle.
