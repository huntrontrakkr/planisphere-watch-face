notes: Four three-cell petals pointing to the corners, joined by a single centre stitch. In the sampler it fills an L-shaped corner frame four cells thick: an outer solid line, a two-cell "ladder" (filled every other cell, so it shows a row of short gaps) and an inner solid line; along the bottom the ladder reads, top to bottom, ######## / #.#.#.#. / #.#.#.#. / ######## (period 2). The frame is open at the top and its right side is the first upright of the Greek key (bk-1968-11-greek-key-band). The frame itself was not charted cell by cell (the canvas is sheared near the corner).
