title: Oak sprig in a lacis panel (two lobed leaves and a flower head on a diagonal stem)
section: Seasons and plants
source: Federico de Vinciolo, Les Singuliers et Nouveaux Portraicts, Paris 1588 edition (woodcut lacis patterns); The Met 18.68(1-93), Rogers Fund 1918, Public Domain
short-source: Vinciolo 1588 (Met DP364069)
page: image DP364069 (signature Q.ij, "Ce coin de mouchoir contient 33. mailles."), the framed top-right quarter of the handkerchief corner: rows 1-35, columns 37-71 of vinciolo1588-handkerchief-corner-71; crop x1130-1975 y280-1135
url: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/358422
image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/original/DP364069.jpg
size: 35 x 35 cells (the sprig itself spans all of it, from the stem's foot in the bottom-left corner)
symmetry: none
colours: # = darned cell (blank paper in the print: the design), . = open mesh (printed as black squares)
use: season (autumn: oak leaves; or summer: a flowering branch) / corner (it is drawn to sit in a square, stem from the corner)
status: VERIFIED
verified: read with the whole corner (white-cell lattice reader on the inverted image; window darkness at each site and a solid-patch test, two independent readings): within this quarter the two readings agree on 1225/1225 cells, darned cells at most 0.242 darkness and mesh cells at least 0.432. Overlay: images/vinciolo1588-flowering-oak-sprig-35_ver.jpg (green dot = darned cell)
notes: A straight diagonal stem rises from the bottom-left corner; a lobed leaf stands upright on the left (rows 2-19, columns 2-14, with a white midrib down column 8), a second lobed leaf lies along the bottom right (rows 22-34, columns 15-35, with a white midrib along row 28), and a flower head with a checkered centre and spiky petals sits at the top right on two thin tendrils. The leaves are oak-like (stepped lobes); at 1x the upright leaf alone (about 13 x 19) reads as an oak leaf. As drawn, the sprig is the darned work on a dark net; for ink on cream use '#' as ink.
