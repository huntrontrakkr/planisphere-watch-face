size: 23 x 17 cells (with the 3-cell stem of the plant; the urn alone 23 x 14)
symmetry: nearly mirror left-right about the stem (col 11): the bowl, the pierced band and the feet are symmetric; as stitched the left handle ring is squeezed (its hole one cell wide) and the right leg has a one-cell kink (row 14)
colours: # = red cross stitch (the small stitch of this sampler, about 13.4 x 13.1 px), . = ground
use: season (a garden urn to hold any flower sprig: put a seasonal sprig on the stem); village (garden ornament)
status: VERIFIED
verified: small-stitch lattice fitted to the weave on a crop rotated by -2 degrees (the cotton is skewed here: without the rotation the right handle reads a row higher than the left), red channel: 389/391 cells agree (images/bk-1980-63-flower-urn_ver.jpg). The two extra inked cells under the left handle (row 7, cols 2 and 4, values 0.66-0.73) are the lower halves of the handle's bottom-row stitches spilling below the lattice row (checked at 7x zoom), not stitches
notes: A broad rim (row 3) with ring handles at both ends, a band pierced by six openings (rows 7-8), a bowl tapering in steps to a 7-cell foot, and three splayed single-stitch legs ending in forked feet. Reads at 1x as a classical urn. The carnation plant above it (stem, a pair of leaf sprigs, four carnation heads, two leafy side shoots and a central bud) is about 60 x 60 small stitches: too large for the face at 1x and not charted here.
