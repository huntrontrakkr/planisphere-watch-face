Agreement (chart cells incl. a 1-cell empty margin, in frame): & 53/54, 1 42/44, 2 86/88, 3 60/60, 4 99/99, 5 58/60, 6 51/54, 7 61/63, 8 59/60, 9 53/54. All mismatches are stroke ends that the reader puts just under or over threshold (values 0.42-0.66): kept as stitched by eye: the &'s top-left (0.42), the 1's top flick and the top of its stem (0.43, 0.42), the 2's hook (charted one row higher than the reader's 0.58 cell, the stitch straddles both), the 5's stem top (0.42), the 6's left side (0.46, 0.46), the 8's top-right (0.40), the 9's top-left (0.44); left out: fuzz under the 5 and the 6 (0.50, 0.54) and the upper half of the 7's bar (0.66, 0.50: the bar is worked one thread higher than the rest, straddling two rows; charted as one row).
notes: Lining (not old-style) cursive figures of a New York schoolgirl, stitched in black silk over two threads of a coarse linen (the stitches read as clean crosses). The 1 is a stem with a flick at the top and a foot turning left; the 2 has a hook, a round shoulder, a diagonal and a flat base with an upturned end; the 3 has a spur at the end of each arc; the 4 is open-topped with an 8-cell bar; the 5 is an S-like hook and bowl with no top bar; the 6 has an ascender and a gap where its stroke steps two rows (as stitched); the 7 has a 4-cell bar and a long diagonal; the 8's upper loop is cut at the top-left (as stitched). The "10" that ends the row runs into the sampler's border and the lattice breaks there: not charted. The baseline of the original wavers by up to about half a row; the line below sets every glyph on one baseline with one empty column between glyphs (the sampler leaves 2-4).
