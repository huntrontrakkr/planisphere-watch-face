verified: two independent reads of the as-printed box (second block below, with the band's checker fillers): (1) darkness at each white-cell-lattice site, Otsu threshold (g3check.py): 344/345 cells agree; the one difference is a white cell with an ink speck at its centre (band row 5, col 30; its mirror cell is clean white), checked by eye at 6x zoom; (2) white-cell lattice reader (nwtool verify --g3): 341/345, the other three being merged double white cells (row 4 cols 23 and 25, row 10 col 38) that the blob reader marks black and the darkness reader and the eye read white. Overlay: images/quentel1529-lily-pot-23x15_ver.jpg (green = chart cell confirmed black, blue square = the speck)
notes: A central stem carries a 3-5-3 diamond bud; two side stems carry upward triangular buds, and from each bud a leaf hangs outward and up; all three stems stand in a 9-7-5-3-1 inverted triangle (a pot or calyx). In the band the lily stands between checker half-diamonds (top and bottom, both sides), the filler used between the motifs; they are in the second block. At 1x it reads as a stiff lily or fleur; for a narrower piece, drop the leaves' outer two columns (rows 5-9) by hand.
