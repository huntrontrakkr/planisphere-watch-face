size: period 4 cells x 5 rows
symmetry: mirror left-right (axes at columns 0 and 2 of the period), mirror top-bottom, rot180 (bandsym.py)
colours: # = cross stitch (one colour), . = canvas ground
use: ring band (a 5-row ladder: rungs every 4 cells with a crosslet between; reads as a row of linked plus signs)
status: VERIFIED
verified: printed chart (cross symbols on a fine printed canvas grid); stitch lattice fitted to the cross symbols (nwtool read, dark channel, pitch 20.97 x 20.40 px), Otsu threshold; the period tiled 13 times (52 x 5) and overlaid: 260/260 cells agree, no mismatches (images/dmc1890-ladder-crosslet-band_ver.jpg)
notes: Two solid rules joined by a rung every 4 cells; the middle row adds one cross either side of each rung, so each rung carries a small plus sign and the gaps between hold a single empty cell. On the plate it runs 13 periods and ends on a rung (both ends are rungs). At 1x it reads as a beaded ladder; the simplest of the DMC bands and fully symmetric, so it turns corners cleanly on a rung.
