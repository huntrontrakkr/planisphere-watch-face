notes: Figures made of single cross stitches set corner to corner, like the lettering of the initials on this sampler. The 1 is a leaning stroke with a flag at the top and a foot turned to the left; the 8 is two stacked 2 x 2 loops with a one-stitch waist; the 5 is drawn as a swan-neck curve (flag hooked to the right at the top, the bowl swinging right then back to a tail curling up on the left), with no upright; the 7 has a 5-cell bar and a 45-degree single-stitch stem. In the original a loose thread end hangs from the foot of the 1 (not charted). Only 1, 5, 7 and 8 occur on this sampler.
