verified: lattice from the linen weave (phase demodulation, /tmp/nw/metsamplers/phaselat.py, about 9 px per thread, stitches over 2 threads); black read on darkness (threshold 0.5): 140/140 cells of the chart with a 1-cell empty margin agree; white read on a whiteness channel (light and unsaturated, threshold 0.6) on the same lattice: 140/140 agree. Checked by eye on 4x and 8x zooms and on the overlays (images/met-1993-100-dog_ver.jpg: left black, right white). Caveat: the head and collar are worked one thread lower than the body's grid (half a stitch), so the snout (a horizontal stroke in the original) falls between rows and reads as the diagonal pair K at row 2 col 1 and row 3 col 0; the collar's lower row (two W) is a half-covered row (whiteness 0.6).
notes: A long, low black dog (or cat) with pricked ears, a white bib/collar, four legs and a tail curling up and back over the rump; the sampler has a second, similar animal after the lowercase alphabet (not charted: its frame is crowded by letters and the border). Legible at 1x as a silhouette; for one ink drop the W cells (or make them ground) and it still reads.
