status: VERIFIED
verified: weave lattice on the red channel, pitch held near 10.5 x 10.7 px (the free fit wandered to 10.4 and drifted a cell over three triangles); three periods (33 x 6) against triangles 1-3: 197/198 cells agree. The one miss is the gap cell between triangles 1 and 2 in the top triangle row (value 0.56): a 9x zoom shows it clearly unstitched, the lattice cell there catching the neighbouring crosses. A 9x zoom of the left end shows the rule starting one stitch before the first triangle, as the period has it. Overlay: images/cma-1943-113-sawtooth-band_ver.jpg
notes: Teeth of 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 stitches, each row stepping in one stitch at both ends, hanging from a continuous rule. Triangles 4-6, at the right, are stitched less regularly, with an extra row near the tip (7 rows deep there; no 7-row variant fits them better than 97%), so the period is taken from triangles 1-3. Turn the chart upside down for teeth pointing up.
