verified: two reads on the white-cell lattice (blob reader; darkness at each site, Otsu): both 351/357; the 6 differences are one of the band's small diamond sprigs at the lower right (rows 13-16, cols 18-21), not part of the bird. The two feet (X-shaped toes at the ends of the legs) are lighter than the body but both readers call them filled at this crop's threshold. Looked at the overlay: images/ostaus1567-heron-21x17_ver.jpg
notes: A long beak (the diagonal line from the head down to the right), a hooked neck, a hump-backed body with white spots, one straight leg and one slanting leg ending in small toes.
