use: ring band (a 7-row running wave with a short 7-cell period: reads at 1x as a chain of rolling curls under a heavy top line)
status: VERIFIED
verified: weave lattice on the red channel, fitted per window because the pitch drifts along the band (10.55-10.7 px): curls 1-3 (21 x 7 tiled chart, x1600-1850) 147/147 cells agree; curls 4-8 (35 x 7, x1830-2245) 245/245, and the same 245/245 with the window moved 10 px either way. One lattice over the whole band gave 385/392, the 7 misses all at threshold (0.40-0.49) in curls 1-3 where that single lattice has drifted a third of a cell. Overlay (both windows): images/cma-1943-113-curl-band_ver.jpg
notes: Each curl is a 7 x 7 roll: a heavy 2-row top line (4 then 6 stitches) that runs down the right side into the next curl, and below it an open ring of single stitches with a one-stitch inner hook, so the band reads as a breaking wave or a row of C-scrolls joined at the top. The band begins and ends with a full curl; the reader also finds one more stitch column after the last curl in rows 3-4 (a closing stub, not charted). One of the two shortest-period bands in the library.
