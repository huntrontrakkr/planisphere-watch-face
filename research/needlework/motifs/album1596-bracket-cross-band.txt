symmetry: mirror left-right (about the gap between the brackets and about the red cross) and top-bottom
colours: o = orange, k = black ink, b = pale blue, r = red, . = paper. Face palette: o gold, k ink, b window blue, r red
use: ring band (a wide, rich band; rows 1-2 alone, orange over orange-and-black dots, are a 2-row beaded rule)
status: VERIFIED
verified: grid-paper reader for the cell centres, median colour per cell classified to five reference colours; the period tiled over the drawn band (38 x 14) matches 518/532 cells. The 14 others: 11 cells of the orange rules painted darker (RGB about 150,105,60, read as red; they are orange, not the red of the crosses at 160,80,75), 2 cells at the band's cut left end (the first cross is drawn without its middle), 1 orange drip below a bracket. notes: Each bracket pair is ] [ in blue (single-cell lines) with a black L-shaped block tucked into each inner corner; between pairs, a red cross: a 3-cell bar at top and bottom and a 3x2 block in the middle. Bold and heraldic; at 14 rows it suits a wide card border more than the narrow ring.
