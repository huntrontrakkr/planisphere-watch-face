size: period 6 x 6 rows (the two rules included); stitched 40 cells long (six periods and four cells)
symmetry: mirror left-right about every stem (cols 1 and 4 of the period); the standing T is the hanging T turned upside down and shifted half a period (glide reflection), so the band reads the same upside down
colours: # = red cross stitch, . = cream canvas ground
use: ring band (a framed fret: the rules give the ring a clean inner and outer edge; 6 rows)
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice fitted to the stitches (red channel, pitch 19.95 x 19.44 px): six tiled periods (36 x 6) 216/216 cells agree; the whole band as stitched (40 x 6) 240/240; a second crop further left confirms that the band starts with the first stem and nothing before it. images/ch-18616463-t-fret-band_ver.jpg
notes: From the top rule a stem drops two cells to a three-cell bar (a hanging T); from the bottom rule a stem rises to a bar (a standing T); the bars of the two kinds sit on adjacent rows (rows 3 and 4) and overlap by half, so the band reads as a continuous interlocking fret with one-cell gaps throughout. On the sampler it begins with a hanging stem joined to both rules (col 1 rows 1-4, then the bar runs right) and ends on the stem of a standing T whose bar is cut by the end of the band. Drop the rules for a 4-row fret.
