size: 7 x 7 cells (on the plate each cell is drawn as a 2x2 block of stitches, i.e. the ornament is set at double size beside the letters)
symmetry: full square symmetry (both mirrors, both diagonals, quarter turn)
colours: # = stitch (printed blue-grey), . = ground
use: weather (snowflake, winter) / star (a small sparkle) / corner
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice fitted to the drawn blocks (13.4 px), dark-channel reading; overlay 49/49 cells agree (images/petit1819-snowflake-7_ver.jpg)
notes: A hollow centre, four arms of two cells, each arm ending in a fork, and four single sparks on the diagonals. Six-fold in spirit but drawn four-fold, as needlework snowflakes are. Reads at 1x as a snow crystal; at 2x (as printed) it is a bold winter ornament.
