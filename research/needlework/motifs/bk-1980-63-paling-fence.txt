notes: Boards 4 cells wide in courses two rows high, divided by one-cell gaps; a single stitch sits at every gap crossing, so at 1x the fence reads as a woven hurdle with a bead at each joint. Each panel ends in a 3-row point (2 x 2 shoulders and a one-cell tip). The post is a flower (or clipped tree) head: a 9 x 5 lozenge with three spikes on top and a 3 x 2 neck, on a one-cell stem that runs down between the panels to the third course; it is charted on its own as bk-1980-63-flower-head. A base row closes the fence. The gate (two 2-cell posts and three rails) stands to the right of col 15.
