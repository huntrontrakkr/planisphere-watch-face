title: Bead band: blue lozenges with orange bars, between black forked-serif columns and rose rules (four colours)
section: Narrow bands
source: Manuscript album of designs for lace and embroidery, German, c. 1596 (woodcut grid paper, designs inked and painted by hand); The Met 53.566.7(1-69), Public Domain
short-source: German album c.1596 (Met DP356737)
page: image DP356737 (a page of coloured band and spot patterns), top band; crop x1380-2100 y1210-1510 of the full scan
url: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/368249
image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/original/DP356737.jpg
size: period 8 cells x 12 rows (10 rows between the two rules)
symmetry: mirror left-right (about the black column and about the lozenge's centre) and top-bottom
colours: r = rose red (rules), k = black ink, b = blue-green (verdigris), o = orange, . = paper (cream). Face palette: r red, k ink, b the window blue, o gold
use: ring band (a jewelled band: beads strung between columns; the black columns alone are I's with forked serifs)
status: VERIFIED
verified: cell centres from the printed grid (grid-paper reader), each cell's median colour classified to the nearest of five reference colours; the chart (29 x 12 as drawn) matches 343/348 cells; the 5 others are black dots drawn as rings with white centres (read as blue or red), confirmed black by eye on the 2x overlay (images/album1596-oval-bead-band_ver.jpg, red dots).
notes: Painted by hand on printed woodcut grid paper, so the colours are the album's own: verdigris, orange, rose and black. On the page the band starts with a half column at the left (no left serifs) and runs 3.5 periods.
