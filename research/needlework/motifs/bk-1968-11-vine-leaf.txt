verified: lattice fitted to the stitches (pitch about 23.2 x 23.8 px), red channel: 165/169 cells agree; the 4 others are stitches of the neighbours deliberately left out of the chart: the end of a tendril above the leaf (row 1, cols 4-5) and the tip of the next leaf at the lower left (row 13, cols 1-2) (images/bk-1968-11-vine-leaf_ver.jpg)
notes: A solid three-lobed leaf: the right lobe ends in a point at col 12 with a one-cell notch below it (row 6), the broad lower lobe runs down to a point at the bottom right, and a one- to two-cell slit at the foot (rows 12-13) separates the small lower-left lobe. The stalk (rows 3-4, cols 1-2) is the start of the stem it hangs from. In the border the leaves alternate along a zigzag stem with spiral tendrils (single-stitch lines) and are drawn in several slightly different shapes; this one was chosen for its clean outline. Wool on canvas, solid fill; at 1x it reads as a leaf silhouette.
