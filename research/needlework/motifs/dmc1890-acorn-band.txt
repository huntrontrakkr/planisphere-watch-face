symmetry: none (the acorn leans to the right); the same band is printed again under the oak band rotated 180 degrees (hanging acorns)
colours: D = dark-shaded cross symbol (the nut and its forked tip), M = medium-shaded cross (the netted cup, the stalk, the rule), L = light-shaded cross (the second acorn and the curled buds), . = canvas ground. The plate gives tones, not colour names
use: season (autumn: an acorn; for an oak sprig pair it with an oak leaf; with dmc1890-acorn-10 a growth pair, 10 and 17 cells) / ring band (the acorn border, 19 rows, for a wide autumn sector)
status: VERIFIED
verified: printed chart (cross symbols on a fine printed canvas grid); stitch lattice fitted to the cross symbols (pitch 20.97 x 20.82 px); a cell counts as inked when at least 18% of its inner area is very dark (light-shaded symbols score 0.22-0.33, ground at most 0.12; my variant of nwtool verify, /tmp/nw/printed/bin/vverify.py). The 40-cell period overlaid on the first two acorns: 760/760 cells agree, and all 280 lettered cells match their tone class (D >= 0.70, M >= 0.45). The 20-cell unit tiled across the whole border (112 x 19): 2105/2128, the 23 misses all in the last, light acorn at the page edge, which prints paler (0.13-0.17). All five acorns on the border are identical in shape, and the hanging border at the foot of the plate holds the same acorn rotated 180 degrees, cell for cell (3 complete repeats, 0 differences). Overlay: images/dmc1890-acorn-band_ver.jpg
notes: A ripe acorn leaning to the right: a solid dark oval nut with a small forked tip at its upper right, sitting in a cup drawn as a net of single crosses (diamond meshes: the scales), from which a one-cell stalk runs down to the rule. On the plate the acorns alternate: a dark-nutted one (D nut, M cup), then the same acorn all in the light tone; between them a small curled bud (L) stands on its own stalk. At 1x the acorn reads at 17 x 15: the solid nut and the netted cup stay distinct. The same drawing at 10 x 10 (nut, tip, two-symbol cup) is dmc1890-acorn-10 (Pl. 37). Corrected 2026-10-14: the solid part is the nut and the net the cup (the stalk joins the net).
