notes: The canonical counted grape bunch: eight round berries in rows of 2, 3, 2 and 1, each berry a 3 x 2 block with a single cell above and below, so neighbouring berries touch only at their tips and a ground cell is left between them (the holes read as the highlights between the grapes). The two bunches on the sampler hang from tendrilled green stalks on either side of a central vine stem. At 1x it reads at once as grapes; for a smaller bunch drop the top row of berries.
