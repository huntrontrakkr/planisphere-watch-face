title: Balusters on a rule (black two-tier vases, white goblets between)
section: Narrow bands
source: Peter Quentel, Ein new kunstlich Modelbuch..., Cologne 1544 (woodcut pattern book); The Met 22.84.1(1-102), Public Domain
short-source: Quentel 1544 (Met DP357065)
page: image DP357065, third band of five; crop x530-1720 y860-1095 of the full scan
url: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/354658
image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/original/DP357065.jpg
size: period 6 cells x 10 rows (9 rows + the solid rule)
symmetry: each baluster mirror left-right; translation along the band
colours: # = black cell of the woodcut, . = white cell (the white spaces between the balusters read as stacked goblets: a figure-ground band)
use: ring band (a 10-row colonnade: balusters, or a row of stylised firs standing on the rule)
status: VERIFIED
verified: darkness at each site of the white-cell lattice (Otsu) against 8 tiled periods: 480/480 cells agree (images/quentel1544-baluster-band_ver.jpg)
notes: Each baluster: a 1-3-3-5 upper tier, a waist (3, 1), a 3-3-5 lower tier, all standing on a full rule; neighbours touch at the widest rows, so the white between them forms 5-3-3-1 goblets. Without the rule (rows 1-9) it is a 9-row band.
