size: x-height 7 rows (1, 2); 3 and 5 hang 6 rows below the line (13 rows), 4 rises 1 and hangs 3 (11 rows), 6 rises 6 rows (13 rows); 7, 8, 9 (next line) are one size larger: x-height 9, 7 and 9 hang 5-6 rows (13 and 15 rows), 8 rises 6 (15 rows). Widths 4-10 cells (1 = 4x7, 2 = 6x7, 3 = 6x13, 4 = 9x11, 5 = 10x13, 6 = 8x13, 7 = 8x13, 8 = 8x15, 9 = 9x15)
symmetry: none
colours: # = brown silk cross stitch over one thread of an open, gauze-like linen, . = linen ground
use: numerals (ring hours, dates): genuine 18th-century old-style figures with ascenders and descenders; the 1 and 2 (7 rows) sit at x-height, so the set works on a 7-row x-height with room above and below
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice from the linen weave by phase demodulation of the thread pattern in a window around each glyph (/tmp/nw/metsamplers/phaselat.py; threads about 9.2-10.4 px across, 11 px down; one stitch per thread crossing), cell = mean darkness at the cell centre against the local background, threshold 0.5; the same reading made independently on the back of the work (DP363106, mirrored). Charts = front reading, with cells the front reads as ambiguous (0.33-0.6) decided by the back, every glyph checked by eye on 5x zooms with the curved lattice drawn. Front agreement (chart with a 1-cell empty margin, in frame): 1 54/54, 2 70/72, 3 120/120, 4 142/143, 5 179/180, 6 149/150, 7 150/150, 8 168/170, 9 183/187 (overlay images/met-57-122-348-numerals_ver.jpg, 1-5 above, 6-9 below). Back agreement (mirrored charts): 1 51/54, 2 70/72, 3 118/120, 4 139/143, 5 176/180, 6 140/150, 7 148/150, 8 158/170, 9 178/187. Choices: 2, the diagonal's third stitch is charted where the back has it (front reads it one cell right: the stitch straddles); 4, the fourth diagonal stitch (front 0.47) kept because the stroke is continuous in the photograph; 9, top-right of the bowl (front 0.36, back 0.99) kept and its top-left (front 0.58, back 0.00, continuous in the photograph) kept; 5, a straddling stitch at the bowl's lower right (front 0.45, back 0.58) left out after the zoom. Caveat: the linen is an open gauze whose threads wander; the silk spreads over about 1.3 threads, so a few stroke ends could be one cell longer or shorter (the back disagrees on 2-12 cells per glyph, mostly at stroke ends and in the 6 and 8, whose back is less clear).
notes: The sampler copies a printer's type specimen: capitals, lowercase, ligatures (ct, ff, fi, fl, ffi, the long-s pairs and ae, oe), punctuation and figures, in four type sizes, signed "Eliza: Dymond Hull F.bridge 1799". These are the second (smaller) figure line, "123456 / 789"; the larger figures (x-height 9: 12 / 3456789) are on lines 3-4 and "1799" in the signature. Genuine hanging figures: 1 and 2 at x-height; 3, 4, 5, 7, 9 descend; 6 and 8 rise. The 1 is a serifed I with a long foot to the right; the 3 has a flat-topped arc, a pointed waist and a long hanging bowl; the 4 is open with a crossbar on the line and a short tail; the 5 has a flag and a big hanging bowl; the 7 has a downturned left serif. There is no 0 in this line. At 1x they read as figures even in one ink; for a 7-row hour ring use 1, 2 as they are and let 3-9 hang and rise as in print.
