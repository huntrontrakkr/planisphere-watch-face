Two extra inked cells at the top (row 0 col 6, row 1 col 7) are the last stitches of the stag's trailing hind leg, which touches the top rail (charted with the stag); one stile cell (row 8 col 3) reads just under the threshold (0.32) because the stile sits a little off the lattice column; the stile is continuous on the image
notes: Two posts two stitches wide (worked in a denser, interlaced stitch on the original) rise one row above the gate; the gate's stiles rise two rows; three one-cell rails; a base row runs under everything and continues as the fence's base. The fence's connector stitches touch the posts from outside at rows 5, 8 and 11. Open, light and very legible at 1x.
