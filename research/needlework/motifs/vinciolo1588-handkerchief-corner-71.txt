title: Handkerchief corner in lacis, "Ce coin de mouchoir contient 33 mailles" (scrolling foliage round a framed oak sprig)
section: Corners and spot motifs
source: Federico de Vinciolo, Les Singuliers et Nouveaux Portraicts, Paris 1588 edition (woodcut lacis patterns); The Met 18.68(1-93), Rogers Fund 1918, Public Domain
short-source: Vinciolo 1588 (Met DP364069)
page: image DP364069 (signature Q.ij), headed "Ce coin de mouchoir contient 33. mailles."; the whole chart, crop x290-1990 y270-2010 (the printed edge column of half-squares at the left and edge row at the foot are left out)
url: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/358422
image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/original/DP364069.jpg
size: 71 x 71 cells (four quarters of 35 x 35 split by a one-cell cross: the vertical line on column 36 runs through the upper half only, the horizontal line on row 36 through the right half only)
symmetry: none
colours: # = darned cell (blank paper in the print: the design), . = open mesh (each open mesh is printed as a black square). Read the other way round, '.' is the ground of a dark net and '#' the white work on it
use: corner (too big for 1x as a whole; the framed oak sprig quarter is vinciolo1588-flowering-oak-sprig-35; the scroll quarters are a source for corner flourishes)
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice from the black squares (white-cell lattice reader run on the inverted image, local affine fits; 72 x 72 sites); two readings per site: (A) darkness of a window at the site, the most of nine offsets up to 0.15 cell, Otsu threshold: cleanly two-valued (darned cells at most 0.263, mesh cells at least 0.430, nothing between); (B) a solid dark patch at least 0.4 cell across within 0.3 cell of the site: A and B agree on 5038/5041 cells; the three others are printed squares with pale flecks that defeat B, checked at 3x zoom: mesh, as A says. The chart is reading A. Overlay: images/vinciolo1588-handkerchief-corner-71_ver.jpg (green dot = darned cell; every black square is undotted)
notes: An L of scrolling foliage (C-scrolls, curling tendrils, lobed leaves, a ring at the lower right) wraps round a framed quarter holding an oak sprig (two lobed leaves and a flower head on a diagonal stem). The heading's 33 mailles is the inside of each quarter. Lacis is darned on a knotted net: the darned cells are the motif. At 1x on the face a quarter (35 x 35) fits a corner; the whole needs 71 px.
