size: period 6 cells x 7 rows (the waves alone: 6 x 3)
symmetry: none within the unit: every wave breaks to the right (translation only). Mirror the chart left-right for waves running the other way
colours: b = blue (painted RGB about 48,88,119; the top rule and the waves are the same blue), n = navy-black (about 6,10,22), . = paper (about 238,238,216). Face palette: b window blue, n ink
use: ring band (7 rows with both rules; the waves alone are a 3-row band; a sea or sky band: waves, or clouds if mirrored top-bottom)
status: VERIFIED
verified: grid-paper reader (g4lib cell centres), median colour per cell classified to paper / blue / navy (+ a grey class for stains); the period tiled over the whole drawn band (66 x 7 cells, 11 repeats) matches 458/462 cells; every painted cell of the chart agrees (242/242). The 4 others: 3 cells under the top rule in one repeat carry a faint blue-grey smudge (RGB 150-205, not a stitch), and the band's last cell on the right (row 4, column 66) is painted blue to close the band. Overlay (left half above, right half below): images/merlo1835-blue-wave-band_ver.jpg
notes: Each wave is a blue parallelogram of 3 + 3 cells leaning right, with a single navy cell (the curl) at its left foot and a navy 3-cell shadow under its right end; a 1-cell blue rule above and a 1-cell navy rule below, each one row clear of the waves. Very clean and legible at 1x; the shortest period on the plate. The two-colour scheme (blue body, dark curl and shadow) maps directly onto window blue and ink.
