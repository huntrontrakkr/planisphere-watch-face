use: ring band (5 rows, two colours: e.g. * = ink, H = red; the rings can carry the hour marks)
status: VERIFIED
verified: the etched grid is hand-ruled (cell widths 14-18 px) and the page is turned about 1.2 degrees, so the cells were taken between the detected grid lines (columns) and on slanted row lines (rows measured at four places along the band); all three repeats (42 x 5 cells): ink in each cell (Otsu) 210/210 agree with the chart (any symbol = filled); symbol classes by k-means on the cell patches (4 clusters): one cluster is exactly the 12 hatched cells, the others the stars and the empty cells: 210/210. Read by eye first at 5x zoom. Overlay: images/sibmacher1597-hatched-cross-band_ver.jpg (first one and a half repeats; green = symbol cell confirmed)
notes: In each block the stars fill a 9 x 5 rectangle with a diamond-shaped hole holding a ring of four hatched cells round an empty centre; between blocks, two short bars of stars. At 1x the hatched rings read as small lozenges or rosettes in a dotted band.
