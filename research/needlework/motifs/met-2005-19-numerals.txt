notes: The figures of a Westtown Quaker School sampler, worked in one green silk and very evenly spaced: a ready-made period font. Old-style: 1, 2, 0 at x-height; 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 rise (the 3 is the flat-topped "z"-shaped form whose bowl sits on the line); 7 and 9 hang. The 1 has a flag at the top right and a foot at the bottom left; the 2 a hook and an upturned tail; the 4 is open with its crossbar three rows above the line; the 0 is an upright oval, narrower than the oe. The photograph's line rises by about one row from left to right; the line below sets every figure on one baseline with one empty column between figures (the sampler leaves 2-4). At 1x the set reads cleanly in one ink; the x-height 9 suits an hour ring of about 11-12 px with ascenders and descenders.
