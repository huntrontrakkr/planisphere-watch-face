size: period 4 cells x 3 rows (zigzag alone); 4 x 8 with the checker and the frame line as stitched
symmetry: zigzag: each tooth mirror left-right, period 4; the checker has period 2
colours: # = red cross stitch, . = linen ground
use: ring band (a 3-row zigzag; with the checker and rule it is a complete 8-row outer edge for the ring or the card)
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice fitted to the weave (pitch 43.6 x 43.3 px), red channel; 7 tiled periods of the 8-row edge overlaid: 224/224 cells agree (images/tryntje-zigzag-band_ver.jpg)
notes: The zigzag is single stitches set corner to corner, so it is the lightest possible tooth line. Below it, one empty row, a two-row checker (the same checker frames all four sides and runs straight round the corners: see tryntje-corner-battlement), one empty row and the outer rule. Further right the work is crossed by a loose end of red thread (not charted). The running-S band sits one empty row above the zigzag.
