title: Long-tailed bird (peahen or pheasant) in green, gold and brown (24 x 20)
section: Creatures
source: Mary Hall, sampler ("Live to Die" / "The Desire"), England, 1786, silk cross stitch on linen; Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, object 18489537; image via Wikimedia Commons "Sampler (England), 1786 (CH 18489537).jpg", public domain
short-source: Mary Hall sampler 1786 (Cooper Hewitt 18489537)
page: middle of the sampler, the left of the two birds flanking the heading "The Desire" (faces right, towards the tree and the heading); crop x450-850 y1840-2160 of the full CH image (2940 x 4096)
url: https://collection.cooperhewitt.org/objects/18489537/
image: https://images.collection.cooperhewitt.org/230619_6ab5221925289d88_x.jpg
size: 24 x 20 cells (bird 24 wide including the beak; legs and feet in rows 14-18; the tail runs to the bottom-left corner)
symmetry: none (faces right; mirror the chart for a left-facing bird)
colours: g = grey-green silk (outline, back, tail), y = gold silk (breast, neck stripe, tail stripe, eye, beak), b = brown silk (legs and feet), . = linen ground
use: creature (a perched bird for a landscape strip or a corner)
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice fitted to the stitches (pitch 15.1 x 15.2 px), each cell's median colour classified to ground / gold / green / brown: ink against ground 480/480; with the colours 474/480. The 6 colour differences are shades, not shapes: 4 gold cells in the shadowed lower breast and tail stripe read as brown (charted gold, as the stripe runs on unbroken), and 2 half-covered toe cells of the right foot read as gold (charted brown, the colour of the leg). Overlay images/ch-18489537-bird_ver.jpg. The mirror-image bird on the right of the sampler is a free copy (its head sits a row lower), not a cell-exact twin
notes: A plump bird with a small square green head, a gold eye and a two-cell gold beak, a gold stripe down the neck widening into a gold breast, a green back, and a long tail of four diagonal rows (green-gold-green-green) sweeping down to the left; two brown legs with forward feet. 138 stitches. Larger than 15 cells; at 1x it still reads as a bird thanks to the long diagonal tail. A two-colour version (green and gold, legs in gold) loses nothing.
