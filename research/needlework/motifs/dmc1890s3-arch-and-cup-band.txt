size: period 16 cells x 8 rows (a cup 7 wide, a gap, an arch 7 wide, a gap)
symmetry: mirror left-right (axes at columns 3 and 11 of the period: through each cup and each arch) (bandsym.py); the cup is the arch turned upside down
colours: D = dark-shaded cross symbol (arches), L = light-shaded cross symbol (cups), . = canvas ground. The plate gives tones, not colour names
use: ring band (an 8-row cresting: arches and cups alternate like a row of bells and tulips; in one colour a scalloped edge)
status: VERIFIED
verified: printed chart (cross symbols on a fine printed canvas grid); stitch lattice fitted to the symbols (pitch 21.03 x 20.46 px); a cell counts as inked when at least 60% of its inner area is inked (luminance < 170; the light symbols at the right end print pale, so the very-dark rule was not used) (/tmp/nw/printed/bin/vverify.py --useany 0.6); the period tiled across the whole band (55 x 8: four cups, three arches): 440/440 cells agree (images/dmc1890s3-arch-and-cup-band_ver.jpg). Tones split with a sliding 26-column window (2-means, /tmp/nw/printed/bin/tone2.py): the tiled letters agree in 440/440 cells
notes: Each arch is a solid stepped pyramid (a one-cell finial over rows of 3, 5 and 7 cells) that opens below into a stepped notch 1, 3 and 5 cells wide between legs thinning from 3 cells to 1, like a bell or a Gothic arch; each cup is the same shape upside down (two horns, a stepped V, a one-cell foot). On the plate the band starts and ends with a cup. Read as a cresting, the arches point outward; turned over, the cups do.
