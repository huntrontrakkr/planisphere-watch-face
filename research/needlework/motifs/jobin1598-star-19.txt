title: Eight-pointed star of rhomb petals, with a centre dot (from a star lattice)
section: Stars and sky
source: Bernhard Jobin, Neu kuenstlich Modelbuch, Strasbourg 1598; The Met 53.566.1(1-47), Public Domain
short-source: Jobin 1598 (Met DP364152)
page: image DP364152 (signature M3), upper band (a diagonal strapwork lattice with three stars in its middle row); the middle star, x1170-1570 y540-930 of the full scan
url: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/352224
image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/original/DP364152.jpg
size: 19 x 19 cells
symmetry: full square symmetry (mirror left-right, top-bottom, both diagonals, rot90)
colours: # = black cell of the woodcut (worked), . = white cell. On the page the star is black on a white ground inside the lattice
use: star (a bright-star or compass-rose glyph; the corner star of the card) / corner
status: VERIFIED
verified: two reads on the same white-cell lattice: (1) darkness at each lattice site with an Otsu threshold: 359/361 cells agree; the 2 differences are white cells blotted by an ink spot (darkness 0.52 against a threshold of 0.49). (2) white-cell blob reader: 353/361, the 8 differences all white cells with ink spots or breaks that the blob finder missed; every chart cell lands on black. Looked at the overlay: images/jobin1598-star-19_ver.jpg (green = chart cell confirmed black; blue squares = blotted white cells)
notes: Four upright petals (pairs of 1-to-4-cell rhombs above and below the centre) and four sideways petals (5-cell diagonal bars, three deep) around a single centre cell; the white cross between the petals runs through the centre. The same star repeats three times across the band; the lattice nodes hold small lozenges. At 19 cells it is a large glyph at 1x: a prominent star or a corner piece.
