symmetry: the silhouette is mirror left-right (axis between columns 7 and 8 of the mill); the colours are not (the body is red, lit, on the left and dark, shaded, on the right)
colours: R = red, B = dark (the plate's blue-black), . = ground. Printed in colour on the plate (chromolithograph, filled squares on a printed grid)
use: village (a windmill for the horizon or a corner; at 1x the X of the sails reads at once)
status: VERIFIED
verified: colour reader (/tmp/nw/printed/bin/colread.py): lattice fitted to the colour fills (pitch 13.62 x 13.53 px; the printed grid lines sit about a third of a cell off the fills, a registration offset of the colour plates, so the fills, not the lines, define the cells), each cell classified by its mean colour as ground / red / dark (no ambiguous cells); the chart overlaid: 442/442 cells agree in colour (images/dillmont-alphabete-windmill_ver.jpg)
notes: A post mill: a red body with a dark right side and a dark roof band, a window of 2 x 3 empty cells (the plate prints the window's grid lines dark, as glazing bars), four sails as an X of two-cell strokes ending in 2 x 2 or 3 x 2 blocks, standing on a two-row mound (22 and 26 cells). A thin dark spike above the cap, narrower than a cell, is not a cell and is left out. For one colour use the binary silhouette. Sibling motifs on the same plate: an owl on a branch, two birds face to face, a dog, a fence with palings, a stork, a tree, deer and a hunter.
