title: Bud sprig (a young shoot: a lozenge bud over two slanting leaves, the stem curling at the foot)
section: Seasons and plants
source: Manuscript album of designs for lace and embroidery, German, c. 1596 (woodcut grid paper, filled in by hand); The Met 53.566.7(1-69), Public Domain
short-source: German album c.1596 (Met DP356728)
page: image DP356728 (leaf numbered 56), top right: the left of two small sprigs drawn side by side; crop x1760-2160 y1000-1350 of the full image (2968 x 4000). The right-hand sprig (x2280-2640) is its mirror image (stem curling left), but its outer leaf runs into the gutter, so the left one is charted.
url: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/368249
image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/original/DP356728.jpg
size: 16 x 13 cells
symmetry: mirror left-right about the stem (columns 8-9) in the bud, leaves and node (rows 1-11); only the foot of the stem curls to the right. The page gives the mirrored twin, so it comes as a facing pair.
colours: # = cell marked with an ink dot (one dot per cell), . = empty cell
use: season (spring: a sprouting bud; the facing pair for the two sides of the window) / village (a garden seedling)
status: VERIFIED
verified: regular-lattice reader snapped to the woodcut grid lines (pitch 21.5 x 22.2 px), each cell read as dot contrast (85th minus 15th percentile of darkness inside the cell); overlay: all 52 charted cells confirmed, 202/208 cells agree. The 6 others are detections on cells that are blank on the scan (paper stains: row 2 col 4 and rows 10-12 cols 1-4), checked by eye on the overlay (images/album1596-bud-sprig_ver.jpg).
notes: Drawn in single ink dots on printed grid paper. A two-cell bud tip, a 4 x 2 bud, a two-cell stem; three-cell leaf strokes slanting down and outward on each side, tied to the stem by one cell; a 4-cell node, and the stem, which steps to the right at the foot. Light, open and legible at 1x: a spring shoot. The c.1596 album draws its small sprigs in pairs like this, ready to face each other.
