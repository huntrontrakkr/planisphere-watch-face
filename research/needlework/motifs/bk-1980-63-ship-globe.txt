notes: An octagonal ring 13 x 9 (top and bottom runs of 7, single-stitch diagonals, 3-row sides) crossed by a full-width bar at mid-height and by three vertical lines: a single line at col 4, the 2-cell mast at cols 6-7 and a single line at col 8 (the mast continues above and below the ring on the sampler, not charted). On the ship it is the main-mast top; out of context it reads as a terrestrial globe with its equator and meridians, which suits the almanac face. For a symmetric globe, fill col 5 or empty col 7 (editorial).
