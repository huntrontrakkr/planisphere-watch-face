size: 20 x 15 cells
symmetry: mirror left-right about the stem (column 10) in the bud, the upper wings and the inner part of the lower wings; the lower wings' outer ends are one cell longer on the right (as engraved)
colours: # = stitch (printed red), . = ground
use: corner (a finial or cresting at the corner of the card) / season (a stylised flower)
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice fitted to the printed squares (6.9 px), red-channel reading; overlay 300/300 cells agree (images/petit1819-fleuron_ver.jpg)
notes: A late descendant of the 16th-century fleurons: a hollow lozenge bud, two stepped wings, a short stem, then a broad pair of wings with diagonal rows of white dots, ending in a 3-cell foot. For a symmetric version drop column 20 of row 8 and the last cell of rows 9-13 on the right.
