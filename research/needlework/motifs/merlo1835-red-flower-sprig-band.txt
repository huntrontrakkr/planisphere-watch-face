title: Sprig band: small orange-and-dark-red flowers in mirrored pairs on a grey-green stem, with dark and grey-green leaves (Berlin work, four colours)
section: Narrow bands
source: Emma Merlo, Neueste Portefeuille für Freundinnen weiblicher Kunst-Arbeiten: eine Auswahl von Guirlanden, allerlei Blumen, Kränzen, Bouquets, Fruchtkörben, Schmetterlingen, Kanten, Arabesken u.s.w. zum künstlichen Stricken, Häkeln, zur Tapisserie- und Mosaik-Arbeit, Pirna/Leipzig: Carl August Friese, c. 1835 (catalogued [183-?]); hand-coloured printed charts ("bei Friese in Leipzig"); Getty Research Institute copy, archive.org neuesteportefeui00merl; public domain (published in the 1830s; no rights statement on the item)

size: period 20 cells x 13 rows (one flower: 4 x 4)
symmetry: mirror top-bottom (about the stem row); no left-right mirror (dark leaves lead, grey leaves follow); translation by 20
colours: g = dark green (painted RGB about 17,28,20; luminance 10-50), s = grey-green (about 58,80,66; luminance 50-106, the stem painted a little denser than the leaves but the same paint), o = orange (about 175,78,19), r = dark red (about 51,9,11), . = paper (about 239,240,222). Face palette: g dark green, s grey-green or light green, o gold-orange, r red
use: ring band (13 rows; a neat, regular flower-and-leaf border, period 20); the flower alone is a 4 x 4 summer spot
status: VERIFIED
verified: grid-paper reader (g4lib cell centres), median colour per cell classified to paper / orange / grey-green / dark green / dark red (references centred on the two green clusters, which separate at luminance about 50; dark cells re-decided by hue between dark green and dark red); the period from the majority of four repeats (the one 2:2 tie, a leaf-base cell under the stem, set by the band's mirror symmetry: its mirror cell is dark green in all four repeats), tiled over the whole drawn band (80 x 13 cells, 4 repeats starting and ending on the stem) matches 1035/1040 cells. The 5 others all sit on the dark/grey-green boundary: the tie cell itself and one more leaf-base cell painted light (RGB about 41-48, 58-64, 39-46), one dark leaf cell in a thin wash (85,97,87), and one stem cell painted dark (10,26,20). Overlay (left half above, right half below): images/merlo1835-red-flower-sprig-band_ver.jpg
notes: A stem along the middle row; at each node a pair of dark green leaves (3-cell blades fanning up and down), then a pair of flowers (each a 3 x 3 orange head with dark red corner cells, one above and one below the stem), then a pair of narrower grey-green leaves. At 1x the flowers read as orange points framed in dark red, the leaves as a herringbone. Probably meant as tiny roses or carnations; for the face, the flower pair plus the stem is a good 13-row ring band, and the 6-row upper half (rows 1-6) a one-sided cresting.
