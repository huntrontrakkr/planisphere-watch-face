section: Stars and sky
source: Sampler, Italy, 1802 (signed F. N. A.), red silk on linen; Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, object 18616385; image via Wikimedia Commons "Sampler (Italy), 1802 (CH 18616385).jpg", public domain

size: 13 x 13 cells (each cell is one star stitch, an eight-armed eyelet over about 4 x 4 threads, so the star is stitched at twice the scale of the letters)
symmetry: full square symmetry (both mirrors, both diagonals, quarter turn)
colours: # = red star stitch, . = linen ground. Face palette: red on cream, or cream on the blue window
use: star (a large star or compass rose; the opening ornament of a line of text, as on the sampler)
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice fitted to the stitch units (red channel, pitch 33.8 x 36.8 px); 169/169 cells agree, no mismatches; images/ch-18616385-star-13_ver.jpg
notes: A Greek cross with a 3 x 3 centre; each arm ends in an open lozenge of four units with the arm's tip beyond it, and two-unit diagonal rays fill the angles. 41 units. Because every unit is itself a little eight-armed star, the sampler's version sparkles; drawn with plain pixels it is a clean 13-cell star. The museum calls these opening ornaments "Maltese crosses". The second alphabet opens with a smaller cousin, and the field holds ch-18616385-star-11 and ch-18616385-cross-star-15.
