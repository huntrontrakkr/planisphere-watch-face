size: period 6 cells x 5 rows
symmetry: mirror left-right about each T (axes at columns 2 and 5 of the period); glide reflection (top-bottom mirror + shift 3 cells); rot180 (bandsym.py)
colours: # = cross stitch (one colour), . = canvas ground
use: ring band (a 5-row fret or battlement filigree; one-cell lines throughout)
status: VERIFIED
verified: printed chart, each stitch drawn as a cross symbol on a fine printed canvas grid (4 x 4 canvas squares per stitch); stitch lattice fitted to the cross symbols (nwtool read, dark channel, pitch 20.97 x 20.19 px), Otsu threshold; the period tiled 9 times (54 x 5) and overlaid on the band: 270/270 cells agree, no mismatches (images/dmc1890-tee-fret-band_ver.jpg)
notes: Between a solid top rule and a solid bottom rule, T's hang from the top rule (3-cell bar, 1-cell stem) and stand on the bottom rule (1-cell stem, 3-cell bar), alternating every 3 cells, so the band reads as an interlocking fret. On the plate the band runs 9 periods and a column. At 1x on the face: a crisp 5-row line of crenellations, the lightest of the DMC narrow borders.
