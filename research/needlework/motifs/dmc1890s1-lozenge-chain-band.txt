status: VERIFIED
verified: printed chart (cross symbols on a fine printed canvas grid); stitch lattice fitted to the symbols (pitch about 20.8 px); a cell counts as inked when at least 30% of its inner area is very dark (/tmp/nw/printed/bin/bandauto.py + vverify.py); the 12-cell period tiled over the whole band (36 x 9): 324/324 cells agree (images/dmc1890s1-lozenge-chain-band_ver.jpg). Tones: two groups (0.50-0.70 and 0.73-0.86); split at 0.72 the tiled letters agree in 317/324 cells, the 7 others light cells reading 0.72-0.78; the first unit checked by eye on a 4x zoom, every cell's tone as in the period
notes: Each unit is a lozenge 9 cells wide and 9 tall outlined by a continuous diagonal line of dark single crosses (touching corner to corner), with a light saltire (an X of 5 cells) at its centre; its top and bottom points sit in gaps of the rule lines, which are broken into light 5-cell pieces, each piece joined to the neighbouring lozenges' sides by one light diagonal cell. At 1x a row of open diamonds on a broken line; the lozenge alone (columns 2-10 of the period) is a 9 x 9 diamond spot motif.
