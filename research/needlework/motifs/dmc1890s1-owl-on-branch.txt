Independent check: the c.1900 colour plate (dillmont-alphabete-owl, Alphabete Tafel 80) prints the same chart: 238/238 cells agree in shape; its colours follow L = red, M and D = blue-black, except one breast spot (row 11, column 8) that the colour plate leaves red
notes: The same owl as dillmont-alphabete-owl, here in its 1890 first printing, in three tones. Ear tufts of one cell over two, a square head with two dark eyes and a dark beak below between them, a body widening to 9 cells with dark spots on a loose diagonal lattice, two dark talons gripping a branch that runs off to the lower left (3 rows, stepping down) and to the right. The plate draws two twigs as straight stitches (lines) from the branch's right end; they are left out.
