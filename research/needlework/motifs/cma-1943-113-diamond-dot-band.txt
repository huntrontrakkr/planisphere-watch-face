title: Chain of outline diamonds with plus-crosses at the centres, tips and junctions, period 14 (red cross stitch)
section: Narrow bands
source: Sampler signed "Maria Hancock", England, 19th century; red cotton cross stitch on cotton, 30.5 x 26.1 cm; The Cleveland Museum of Art 1943.113, Gift of George W. Bierce; CC0 (Open Access, share_license_status CC0)
short-source: Maria Hancock sampler (CMA 1943.113)
page: lower band field, right-hand end of the row of the star chain; crop x3040-3870 y3410-3650 of the full image (1943.113_full.tif, 4050 x 4773), rotated -0.5 degrees for the fabric skew
url: https://clevelandart.org/art/1943.113
image: https://openaccess-cdn.clevelandart.org/1943.113/1943.113_full.tif
size: period 14 x 15 rows (5 diamonds stitched: 73 x 15)
symmetry: the band mirrors left-right and top-bottom (rot180 too); the period as cut (starting at a junction) mirrors top-bottom
colours: # = red cross stitch, . = cream cotton ground
use: ring band (a light open lattice band: single-cell diagonals with five-stitch pluses; 15 rows, or crop rows 2-12 for an 11-row band without the junction pluses)
status: VERIFIED
verified: weave lattice (red channel, pitch 10.6 x 11.5 px) after a -0.5 degree de-skew; the as-stitched chart (5 periods with both end pieces, 73 x 15): 1091/1095 cells agree (the same count at -0.8 degrees). All 4 mismatches are in the last three columns, where the band bends up more steeply: the top-right end plus is stitched one column right of the pattern (a local check on the right end with its own lattice gives the same: its five misses are that plus, one column off). Overlay: images/cma-1943-113-diamond-dot-band_ver.jpg
notes: Diamonds 15 x 15 drawn with single-cell diagonals, touching at their side vertices (an X at each junction); a five-stitch plus marks each diamond's centre and caps its top and bottom vertices, and further pluses stand above and below every junction, so the pluses form a running row along both edges at half-period spacing. As stitched, the band starts and ends with the left/right arms of the end pluses. A cleaner, more open companion to the star chain in the same row.
