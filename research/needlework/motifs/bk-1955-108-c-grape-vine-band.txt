The same chart against the next repeat to the right: 349/380 cells: the hand varies (that repeat's lower unit sits one row higher and some leaf and vine cells shift by one). Colours: green/purple by hue (G-R of the cell mean), checked by eye at 4x and 8x zoom; dark-green stitches that an RGB nearest-colour rule calls purple were corrected by the hue rule
notes: Each unit is a diamond leaf cluster (1-3-5-3-1 rows) on a stalk, with a dark bunch hanging below it under a green cup: the upper unit's bunch is 6 wide x 6 tall (rows 9-14), the lower unit's 5 x 7 (rows 13-19). A one-cell vine zigzags between them, with a pair of small three-cell leaves where it bends. The vine has one gap in the charted repeat (row 10 col 18, under the lower leaf cluster), as stitched. The museum lists the border as grape bunches; on the face it could be cut to rows 3-15 (the upper units only) for a narrower band. Tile horizontally: column 1 continues the vine from column 20.
