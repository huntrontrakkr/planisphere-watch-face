notes: A children's sampler alphabet: figures 5 rows tall, every stroke one cell, curves made of single diagonal steps. The 1 is an I with forked serifs top and bottom (as in the 1596 album figures); the 2 has a flat 4-cell foot; the 3 a one-cell waist; the 4 is open with a 4-cell bar and a forked foot; the 5 and 6 begin with a flag at the top right; the 7 has a hooked bar; the 9 ends in a two-cell tail at the lower left. On the plate a full stop follows the 0, then a small 4 x 5 ornament (a stepped cross: #.#. / .### / ##.# / .### / #.#.) and further letters.
