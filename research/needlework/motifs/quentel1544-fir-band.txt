title: Row of firs over a beaded sawtooth (black spade-firs with white branch veins)
section: Narrow bands
source: Peter Quentel, Ein new kunstlich Modelbuch..., Cologne 1544 (woodcut pattern book); The Met 22.84.1(1-102), Public Domain
short-source: Quentel 1544 (Met DP357090)
page: image DP357090, the lowest of seven bands; crop x545-1720 y1990-2250 of the full scan (2139 x 2635)
url: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/354658
image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/original/DP357090.jpg
size: period 12 cells x 10 rows (7-row firs + a dotted row + a 2-row sawtooth; on the page a black rule and a white line above and below)
symmetry: each fir mirror left-right (the period is shown with the fir's axis in its 7th column); the dot row has period 3 and the sawtooth period 6
colours: # = black cell of the woodcut, . = white cell
use: ring band (winter: a forest of firs; or village: a hedge of trees) / the fir alone (rows 1-7, 11 x 7) as a small tree
status: VERIFIED
verified: darkness at each site of the white-cell lattice (Otsu) against 5 tiled periods: 600/600 cells agree (images/quentel1544-fir-band_ver.jpg)
notes: Each fir grows 1-3-5 cells from its tip, then spreads in two white-veined tiers to an 11-cell base that touches its neighbours; under the row of firs a line of single cells every third column and a small sawtooth (3-cell and 5-cell steps) on the rule. A ready winter or forest band at 1x.
