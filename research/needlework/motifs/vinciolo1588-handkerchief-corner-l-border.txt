title: Handkerchief corner in lacis, "contient en largeur 35 mailles" (an L-shaped border of scrolls and checker round the plain corner of the cloth)
section: Corners and spot motifs
source: Federico de Vinciolo, Les Singuliers et Nouveaux Portraicts, Paris 1588 edition (woodcut lacis patterns); The Met 18.68(1-93), Rogers Fund 1918, Public Domain
short-source: Vinciolo 1588 (Met DP364070)
page: image DP364070 (signature Q.iij), headed "Ce coin de mouchoir contient en largeur 35. mailles."; the whole chart without the printed edge column (left) and edge row (foot); crop x250-1960 y280-2030
url: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/358422
image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/original/DP364070.jpg
size: 70 x 72 cells: a darned frame line down column 1 and along row 72; the L-shaped border is columns 2-40 (top part, rows 1-31) and rows 33-71 (foot part; its last two rows plain); the plain open-mesh square of the cloth (columns 42-70, rows 1-31) is framed by darned lines on column 41 and row 32
symmetry: none (the border runs on past the top and right edges of the chart: a running border turning the corner)
colours: # = darned cell (blank paper in the print: the design), . = open mesh (each open mesh is printed as a black square)
use: corner (the turning of a border round a corner: scrolls in the angle, checker panels along the arms; too big for 1x as a whole, a source for corner flourishes)
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice from the black squares (white-cell lattice reader on the inverted image, local affine fits; 71 x 73 sites); two readings per site: window darkness (the most of nine offsets up to 0.15 cell, Otsu) and a solid-patch test (a dark patch at least 0.4 cell across within 0.3 cell): over the 70 x 72 chart they agree on 5040/5040 cells (darned at most 0.290, mesh at least 0.451). One cell was corrected by eye: row 72, column 1, where the frame lines meet, is blank paper; both readers caught the top of a deformed square of the edge row below it (the lattice drifts at that corner), checked at 3x zoom. Overlay: images/vinciolo1588-handkerchief-corner-l-border_ver.jpg (green dot = darned cell)
notes: In the angle, big C-scrolls and spirals; along the top arm a vase-like panel of chevrons and a three-row cable (rows 20-23); along the foot arm, panels of checker and stepped crosses. The heading's 35 mailles is the width of the border. As drawn, the design is the darned work on a dark net; for ink on cream use '#' as ink.
