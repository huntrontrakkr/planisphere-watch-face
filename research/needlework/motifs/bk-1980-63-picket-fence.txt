use: village (a garden or churchyard paling on the horizon); a vertical-stripe ring band if cut short
status: VERIFIED
verified: small-stitch lattice fitted separately in x and y (the weave is not square here: 12.8 x 14.2 px; a variant of nwtool with a separate y pitch), red channel; 13 x 20 window (6 pales and their gaps, tips to base): 256/260 cells agree (images/bk-1980-63-picket-fence_ver.jpg). The 4 extra inked cells are at the second gap just under and above the rail (values 0.45-0.71), where a stitch of the rail is doubled where it meets the tall end panel
notes: Single-stitch pales two cells apart, each rising one stitch above a continuous rail that runs one row below the tips; a base row closes the fence. At the left end a taller panel of four close-set pales with a capped top rises 6 rows higher, and a gate post with a two-stitch cap (where the bird stands) interrupts the run on the right; neither is charted. At 1x the run reads as fine vertical hatching: the lightest fence in the library.
