verified: lattice fitted to the stitches (pitch 24.1 x 23.7 px; the canvas weave is 12.2 x 11.6 px per thread), read in the blue-absorption channel (255 - B, which separates both gold and brown from the cream ground): filled/empty 499/500 cells agree; the one mismatch is the leg tip at row 5 col 5, a half stitch (images/bk-1968-11-bee_ver.jpg). Colours by nearest-colour classification of the cell means: 488/500 agree; the 12 others are half stitches or fluff (antenna and leg tips read lighter, five faint gold part-stitches at wing edges, two cells on the brown/gold border), checked by eye at 3x zoom
notes: Two antennae (rows 1-4) and two forelegs (rows 5-7) as single-cell diagonals; head and thorax striped brown and gold; abdomen of brown and gold stripes with small cream windows between them, ending in a gold sting (rows 19-20); wings of brown and gold columns, each with a gold diagonal vein running out from the top. Half stitches (not charted) round off the wing tips and finish the antenna, leg and vein ends. In ink alone (b and y both '#') it reads as a striped insect.
