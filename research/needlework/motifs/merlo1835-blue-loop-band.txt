notes: A double rule (black, then blue with a small three-step bump at each loop) on one side and a plain blue rule on the other; between them a blue ribbon drawn as a series of loops: a 5-cell blue bar, a black shadow bar under it (5 cells, then 2, then 1, stepping down), a blue loop back. The black shadows give the ribbon a twist and read at 1x as a rope or ribbon; a good companion to the blue wave band (merlo1835-blue-wave-band).
