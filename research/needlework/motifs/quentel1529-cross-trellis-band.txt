section: Narrow bands

short-source: Quentel 1529 (Met DP361383)

size: period 8 cells x 15 rows
symmetry: no mirror; the lower row is the upper row turned through 180 degrees (half-turn symmetry about the middle of the zigzag)
colours: # = black cell, . = white cell
use: ring band (a wide one: 15 rows; the middle 5 rows alone, rows 6-10, are a plain zigzag stem)
status: VERIFIED
verified: 6 tiled periods (48 x 15) overlaid on the right part of the band: darkness at the white-cell-lattice sites (g3check.py) 716/720 agree; white-cell lattice reader (nwtool verify --g3) 712/720. The differences: one site at the crop's left edge where the lattice is extrapolated (row 6, col 1 of the tiled chart; the cell is confirmed in the other five repeats), and three isolated extra black cells (rows 2 and 10), each in one repeat only (blots or cutting faults; the block is worn here); g3 adds four merged double white cells. Overlay: images/quentel1529-cross-trellis-band_ver.jpg (first three periods)
notes: A zigzag stem runs along the middle (rows 6-10); from each upper point a one-cell stalk climbs to a small cross (a 3 x 3 block with single-cell arms, the vertical arm on the block's right column, so the cross leans with its stalk), and from each lower point a stalk drops to the same cross turned upside down. At 1x it reads as crosses on a trellis. The left third of the band on the page reads one column off in the upper rows: the woodcut grid is distorted there (the lattice reader's columns 17 and 18 collapse), so it was not used.
