size: period 8 cells x 5 rows
symmetry: each merlon mirror left-right; period 8 (merlon 5 wide, gap 3)
colours: # = red cross stitch, . = linen ground
use: ring band (a castle-wall line round the ring; also an inner rule of the card)
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice fitted to the weave, red-channel reading; 3 tiled periods overlaid: 118/120 cells agree; the two mismatches are one corner stitch the embroiderer set a cell to the right (images/tryntje-battlement-band_ver.jpg)
notes: A single-line meander: only one cell wide everywhere, so it stays light. At 5 rows it fits a narrow ring band; it turns the corner of the sampler cleanly (see the top-left corner of the sampler).
