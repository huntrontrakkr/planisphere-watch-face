title: Star on a flowering staff (eight-rayed star over a staff with buds and two scrolls)
section: Stars and sky
source: Manuscript album of designs for lace and embroidery, German, c. 1596 (woodcut grid paper, filled in by hand); The Met 53.566.7(1-69), Public Domain
short-source: German album c.1596 (Met DP356671)
page: image DP356671, the LUNA page, left edge beside the figure; crop x1000-1340 y1380-1900
url: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/368249
image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/original/DP356671.jpg
size: 11 x 22 cells (the star alone, rows 1-10: 10 x 10)
symmetry: nearly mirror left-right about the staff (the 6th column); the star's horizontal arm is one cell longer on the right, the base one cell longer on the right, and one cell of the star's upper left is lost to a paper repair
colours: # = cell filled with ink, . = empty cell
use: star (a star-topped finial or pointer; the star alone as a small eight-rayed star) / weather (a vane-like finial)
status: VERIFIED
verified: grid-paper reader, darkness of every cell printed on a 2.4x zoom and read by eye (filled 0.5-0.6, empty 0.1-0.25); overlay 242/242 (images/album1596-luna-star-staff_ver.jpg). Two small white paper repairs sit at row 3-4 left of the staff; the cells beside them are read as they are.
notes: An eight-rayed star (the rays one cell wide, the diagonals two or three cells), a long staff with a pair of buds, two curled scrolls like a fleur-de-lis foot, and a 6-cell base. Single-cell lines throughout: light and legible at 1x.
