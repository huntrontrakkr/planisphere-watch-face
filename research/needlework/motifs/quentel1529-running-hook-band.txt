verified: the woodcut's grid lines are unevenly spaced (gaps of 9-32 px for a nominal 20 px unit in x, about 21 px in y) and its lines wobble by up to 6 px, so a regular lattice cannot fit. The lattice lines were placed on the vertical-stroke and horizontal-stroke peaks of the image without using the chart (eight vertical strokes per repeat, one per lattice line, matching the stroke counts expected), every unit edge was sampled on stroke maps, and the chart compared edge by edge over three repeats: 217/220 unit edges agree. The three others: a real break in the third repeat's bottom line, a corner where the line overshoots by a few px (first repeat, bottom line), and the next repeat's first edge at the right of the crop. The topology (which edges are drawn) is the same in all three repeats and is unambiguous; only the spacing was regularised. Overlay: images/quentel1529-running-hook-band_ver.jpg (green = chart edge on the woodcut's line, red = the break, blue = image line outside the chart)
notes: Each repeat is a squared spiral (an outer box with a side ear and an inner hook) whose bottom line climbs by four stairs to become the top of the next spiral. The band has seven and a half repeats on the page; the last three are squeezed narrower by the cutter (periods of about 142, 130 and 120 px instead of 158) and were not checked edge by edge. At 1x the 9-row pixel version reads as a running scroll.
