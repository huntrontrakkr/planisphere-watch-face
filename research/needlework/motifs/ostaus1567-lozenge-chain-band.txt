short-source: Ostaus 1567 fol. LXIIII (Met DP362011)

size: period 6 cells x 9 rows (in the woodcut with an empty row and a solid rule above and below)

colours: # = filled cell (cut as a grey-hatched cell in the woodcut), . = white cell
use: ring band (a 9-row chain of diamonds)
status: VERIFIED
verified: the period voted over the band's 21 repeats; 12 tiled periods (with the empty rows) overlaid: 785/792 (blob reader) and 786/792 (darkness at each site); library overlay (6 repeats): 394/396 with both readers. Looked at the overlays: images/ostaus1567-lozenge-chain-band_ver.jpg
notes: Diagonal strokes three cells thick cross to make a chain of small diamonds, each diamond holding a white centre; the triangles left at the top and bottom edges are solid. At 1x it reads as a lattice chain.
