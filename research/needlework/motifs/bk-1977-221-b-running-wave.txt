status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice fitted to the weave (pitch 25.0 x 23.0 px: the cotton weave is not square), dark channel (the blue reads more evenly on darkness than on blue saturation); 5 tiled periods overlaid on units 2-6: 209/210 cells agree (images/bk-1977-221-b-running-wave_ver.jpg); the one extra inked cell is the arm of a neighbouring stitch spilling into a gap (value 0.52)
notes: One continuous line: from the lower left it climbs to a 3-cell top bar, turns down the right side and curls in to the centre, then leaves the centre down the left side to a 3-cell bottom bar and climbs diagonally to the next top bar. The units share nothing but the climbing diagonal, so the band reads as a row of breaking waves or spirals; the first unit on the sampler starts with a closed hook and the last ends in a tail. At 6 rows it is one of the lightest bands in the library.
