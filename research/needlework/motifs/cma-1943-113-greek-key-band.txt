use: ring band (the narrowest true meander in the library: lines and gaps one cell each, 7 rows; crisp at 1x)
status: VERIFIED
verified: weave lattice (red channel) fitted after de-skewing the crop by -1.7 degrees (pitch 10.6 x 10.7 px); the 7 tiled periods (56 x 7) against the whole band: 392/392 cells agree (the same at -1.5 and -1.9 degrees); with the closing upright added (57 x 7): 399/399. Without the rotation, a lattice forced to 10.56 x 11.34 px gave 162/168 on the first three units, the 6 misses all in the third unit where the skew has lifted the band by half a row. Overlay (rotated): images/cma-1943-113-greek-key-band_ver.jpg
notes: The same hook as the 9-row key of bk-1968-11-greek-key-band, one size smaller: the line climbs the upright (col 1), runs along the top, comes down col 7, turns in for a one-cell spiral (rows 3-5) and leaves down col 3 to the foot, where it runs on into the next unit's upright. Top row has a one-cell gap between units; the foot row a gap at col 2. At the right end the stitcher closed the band with one more upright (rows 1-7) after the 7th unit. Together with the 9-row key this makes a two-size meander set.
