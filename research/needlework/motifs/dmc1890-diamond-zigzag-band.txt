size: period 10 cells x 13 rows (zigzag 10 rows + chain rule 3 rows)
symmetry: mirror left-right (axes at columns 0 and 5 of the period: through each lozenge and each V) (bandsym.py)
colours: X = dark-shaded cross symbol (colour 1: the chain rule), x = light-shaded cross symbol (colour 2: the zigzag), . = canvas ground. The plate prints colours as tones; the Directions (p. 13) leave the colours to the worker
use: ring band (a wide one: 13 rows; rows 11-13 alone make a 3-row chain rule, period 2; rows 1-10 alone a zigzag with lozenges)
status: VERIFIED
verified: printed chart (cross symbols on a fine printed canvas grid); stitch lattice fitted to the cross symbols (nwtool read, dark channel, pitch 20.97 x 20.88 px), Otsu threshold; the period tiled 5.5 times (55 x 13) and overlaid: 715/715 cells agree, no mismatches (images/dmc1890-diamond-zigzag-band_ver.jpg). Tones: the chain-rule crosses read 0.7-0.8 darkness, the zigzag 0.5-0.7; checked by eye at 6x zoom (the rule's symbols are near-black, the zigzag's grey)
notes: A one-cell zigzag line (two cells thick on the diagonals) whose peaks each carry a hollow lozenge 7 cells wide; the V's bottom out on the row just above a chain rule of two solid rows with a row of alternate crosses between (a string of tiny square rings). At 1x the lozenges read as a row of beads on a wave; the chain rule alone (X rows) is the narrowest DMC border, 3 rows.
