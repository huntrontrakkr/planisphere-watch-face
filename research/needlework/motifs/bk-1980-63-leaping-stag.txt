notes: A solid body with nine single open cells (the dappling: linen cells left unstitched) on tucked forelegs folded forward (an open loop on the right, rows 22-25); the hind legs trail behind, the lower one running out as a single-stitch diagonal that ends on the gate's top rail (rows 26-28). The long neck rises at the front; the head is turned back over the body, muzzle to the left (rows 9-12), with one antler climbing to a three-pointed palm (rows 0-8) and the other swept back as a long tine with a forked tip (rows 3-10, right). Large (32 cells), so for the face it suits a wide panel or a 2x-reduced redraw.
