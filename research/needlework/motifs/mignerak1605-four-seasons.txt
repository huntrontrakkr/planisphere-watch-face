notes: What each plate holds, for redrawing the seasons in the face's own pixel art:
  PRINTEMPS: Flora, crowned with flowers, holds up a flowering branch; a chapel with three round-arched windows and a tiled roof (right); a vase of lilies (left); trees in leaf; a large rose (lower left); grass tufts along the ground; a small bird perched at the upper right.
  ESTE: a woman crowned with flowers plays a bass viol; an open music book and a flower spray (lower right); leafy trees; a river bank or field behind.
  AUTOMNE: a figure crowned with fruit and leaves holds a sheaf of wheat (ears at the top, right of centre); bunches of grapes on a vine (left); a large ribbed gourd or pumpkin (lower right); clouds (top left).
  HYVER: an old woman wrapped in a hooded cloak warms her hands at a fire; bare, forked trees; a house with a steep gable and a door (left); a round disc on a stalk above it (a low sun, or a sign).
  For the face, the emblems that reduce well to 7-12 cells are: a blossom spray or rose (spring), a wheat sheaf (summer or autumn), a grape bunch (autumn), and a bare tree or a fire (winter).
