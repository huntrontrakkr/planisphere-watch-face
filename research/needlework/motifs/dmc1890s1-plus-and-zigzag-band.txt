Tones: the inked cells fall in two groups (very-dark fraction 0.50-0.70 and 0.74-0.86); split at 0.72, the tiled letters agree in 320/324 cells, the 4 others in the band's first column, which prints darker; checked by eye on a 4x zoom (the plus signs are solid black crosses, the rest hatched)
notes: Between two light rules a light zigzag of diagonal steps (cells paired on rows 3 and 5) runs from just under the top rule (peaks) to just over the bottom rule (troughs). Each valley holds a dark plus sign (four dark cells round a light centre): above each trough against the top rule, below each peak against the bottom rule, so the pluses alternate top and bottom every 4 cells. On the plate the band begins with a dark vertical bar at the left (an end piece, not part of the period).
