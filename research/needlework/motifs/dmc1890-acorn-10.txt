size: 10 x 10 cells (with the nut's tip and the start of the twig)
symmetry: none (the acorn hangs on a diagonal, tip to the upper left, twig to the lower right; it is close to symmetric about that diagonal)
colours: X = cross symbol (the nut, part of the cup, the twig), o = open square symbol (the other cup cells; a second, lighter colour), . = canvas ground. The border prints its three acorns in three tones of cross (dark, medium, light), all with the same X/o drawing
use: season (autumn: a small acorn, reads at 1x as a dark nut in a chequered cup) / with dmc1890-acorn-band a growth pair, 10 and 17 cells
status: VERIFIED
verified: symbol classifier (/tmp/nw/printed/bin/symcls.py, symver.py): lattice fitted on the acorn's own crop (pitch 20.76 x 20.70 px), each cell classified by the darkness of its centre (cross symbols are dark at the centre) and of its rim (open squares are dark only at the rim); the chart overlaid on all three acorns of the border: 100/100, 100/100 and 99/100 cells agree (the miss is a printing speck above the first acorn) (images/dmc1890-acorn-10_ver.jpg: green dot = cross confirmed, cyan ring = open square confirmed)
notes: A solid 5 x 5 nut (with one cell cut from its lower left corner) with a separate 2 x 2 tip at its upper left, and a cup of crosses and open squares in a diamond net round the nut's lower right, its four gaps left empty, the twig leaving the cup at the lower right. On the plate the acorns hang from a branch with an oak leaf between them. For one colour, make the o cells crosses or leave them out (the nut and the cup's crosses alone still read as an acorn).
