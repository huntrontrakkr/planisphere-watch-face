colours: # = red cross stitch, . = cream cotton ground
use: star (a bold eight-pointed star with a plus-cross centre; at 1x the eight staircase points read clearly) / ring band (the chain of stars)
status: VERIFIED
verified: weave lattice per window (red channel; the weave is uneven, pitch 10.5-11.2 px). The star chart alone on each of the four stars: 223/225, 221/225, 220/225, 223/225; every mismatch is a bar cell (not in the single-star chart) except one borderline cell in star 3 (0-based row 8 col 7, value 0.43 at threshold 0.43). The chain in overlapping pairs (31 x 15 chart: star, bar, star): stars 1-2 464/465, stars 2-3 460/465, stars 3-4 459/465. The misses: incoming-bar cells left of the window (expected), the outgoing bar the pair chart puts after star 4 where the chain ends (expected: image empty), and three cells at threshold (0.31-0.51, a lattice a third of a cell off on uneven weave). 8x zooms of the three junctions show the bar is detached from both stars by a one-cell gap at junctions 2 and 3; at junction 1 the gap next to star 1 is half filled by a stray stitch (value 0.5), charted as a gap. Overlays: images/cma-1943-113-star-chain_ver.jpg (stars 1-2)
notes: Each star: a square of four staircased points (the points at cols 4 and 10 on every side), a full 15-cell bar through rows 4 and 10 and cols 4-10, and an open centre holding a 5-stitch plus. In the chain the stars stand one column apart, joined through the middle row by a 5-cell bar with a one-cell gap at each end. The first star has no bar on its left and the last none on its right. For a single star motif use the 15 x 15 chart; for a running band use the period.
