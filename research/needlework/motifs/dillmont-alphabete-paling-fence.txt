notes: Posts every 4 cells, each topped by a hollow diamond finial (a cell above, one either side, the post below, the centre left open); a top rail on the 4th row and a bottom rail 8 rows lower, the posts running one cell below it onto a red ground row over a dark ground row. On the plate the fence runs from under the hunter's arm to the lamp-post tree; the rails stop at the right one column after a lone finial cell (no fifth post). For a lighter fence drop the ground rows (13 rows).
