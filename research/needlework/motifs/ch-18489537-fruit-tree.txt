short-source: Mary Hall sampler 1786 (Cooper Hewitt 18489537)

url: https://collection.cooperhewitt.org/objects/18489537/

size: 11 x 17 cells (crown 11 x 11, trunk 3 rows, stepped foot 3 rows)
symmetry: mirror left-right
colours: g = grey-green silk, y = gold (yellow-brown) silk (the fruit), . = linen ground. Face palette: green or ink tree, red or ochre fruit
use: village (a tree for a landscape strip); season (an orchard tree in fruit: late summer, autumn)
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice fitted to the stitches (pitch 14.7 x 15.3 px), each cell's median colour classified to ground / gold / green: 187/187 cells agree, no mismatches; images/ch-18489537-fruit-tree_ver.jpg. The right-hand tree is the same design: 183/187 with the same chart, the 4 differences being half-covered cells where the lattice runs half a cell off at the left of the 4th row and the right of the 5th and 7th rows
notes: A tiered crown built of three widening diamonds (a single tip, a 5-wide tier, a 9-wide tier and an 11-wide bottom bar with three drooping tips), seven gold fruits set in the green on the centre line and at the tier ends, a one-cell trunk and a stepped 3-5-7 foot. 77 stitches. Reads at 1x as a small fruit tree or a decorated fir; with the fruit dropped it is a plain tree.
