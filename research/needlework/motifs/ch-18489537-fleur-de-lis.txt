title: Fleur-de-lis in outline (corner piece, gold cross stitch, 21 x 23)
section: Corners and spot motifs
source: Mary Hall, sampler ("Live to Die" / "The Desire"), England, 1786, silk cross stitch on linen; Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, object 18489537; image via Wikimedia Commons "Sampler (England), 1786 (CH 18489537).jpg", public domain
short-source: Mary Hall sampler 1786 (Cooper Hewitt 18489537)
page: top-left corner, inside the strawberry border (one fleur-de-lis sits in each of the four corners); crop x300-660 y370-740 of the full CH image (2940 x 4096)
url: https://collection.cooperhewitt.org/objects/18489537/
image: https://images.collection.cooperhewitt.org/230619_6ab5221925289d88_x.jpg
size: 21 x 23 cells
symmetry: mirror left-right except 4 cells as stitched: row 9 has a 4-stitch top on the right petal against 3 on the left (cell col 20 has no partner at col 2), and row 22 has the left foot one stitch longer than the right. Drop row 9 col 20 and row 22 col 9 (counting from 1) for a mirror-exact version
colours: # = gold (yellow-brown) silk cross stitch, . = linen ground. Face palette: ochre or red on cream
use: corner (a fleur-de-lis for each corner of the card or ring; an almanac printer's ornament)
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice fitted to the stitches (notcream channel, pitch 15.4 x 15.1 px); 483/483 cells agree, no mismatches; images/ch-18489537-fleur-de-lis_ver.jpg. The top-right fleur-de-lis on the same sampler was also read: the same design, but the stitcher varied a few cells (its petals are 3 and 3 at the top and its centre bar is filled), so it is not a cell-exact twin
notes: A single-stitch outline fleur-de-lis: a tall pointed central petal (rows 1-8), two curling side petals that roll inward to a small hook, a crossed band of beads at the waist (rows 12-16) and a three-lobed foot. 102 stitches, all one colour. Open and airy: at 1x it reads as a fleur in outline, best in ochre or red on cream. On the sampler the four fleurs fill the corners inside a reversing strawberry border.
