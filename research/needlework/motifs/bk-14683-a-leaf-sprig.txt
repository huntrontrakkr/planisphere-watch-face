verified: lattice pitch measured on this sprig's stem (19.97 px down) and the ground line (21.3 px across), phase fitted on the gaps; red channel: 326/330 cells agree (images/bk-14683-a-leaf-sprig_ver.jpg). The 4 extra inked cells all sit just right of the stem (values 0.46-0.57): at 6x zoom they are the stem's slanting stitches spilling over the cell edge and, in one place, a loose pink strand, not stitches
notes: An inverted-trapezoid cup flower (7, 5, 5, 3 cells) on a one-stitch stem; each leaf pair is two slanting 4-cell strokes stepping down to a 9-cell crossbar on the stem, and three cells (a node) cross the stem two rows above the second pair. On the sampler the stem goes on with two more leaf pairs (the lower ones stitched less evenly) to a small mound on the ground line, 38 rows in all. A similar sprig, with a bird on its flower head, stands at each side of the rabbits (see bk-14683-a-bird-on-flower).
