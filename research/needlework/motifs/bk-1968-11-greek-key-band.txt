The same 22 x 9 chart on a stretch further right (x3610-4250, y5343-5608): 193/198, the 5 others again fluff on gap cells (overlay looked at); four other stretches give 185-190/198 (counts only; the canvas is uneven and the band drifts 50 px down across the sampler)
notes: Each unit is a hook: the line climbs the left upright (col 1), runs along the top, comes down the right side and spirals in (rows 3-7) and back out down col 3 to the foot, where it runs on under the 1-column gap into the next unit's upright: one continuous line. The foot row has a single gap (col 2) where the upright and the returning line stand side by side. At the far left the band turns up into a corner block (an L-shaped frame with a ladder of dashes holding a four-petal cross). Wool on coarse canvas, so the gaps look narrower than the lines in the photo; as charted, lines and gaps are each one cell.
