title: Cross-star with lozenge ends and diagonal rays (compass rose; red cross stitch, 15 cells)
section: Stars and sky
source: Sampler, Italy, 1802 (signed F. N. A.), red silk cross stitch on linen; Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, object 18616385; image via Wikimedia Commons "Sampler (Italy), 1802 (CH 18616385).jpg", public domain
short-source: Italian sampler 1802 (Cooper Hewitt 18616385)
page: lower field, the left of the two large crosses (between the crowned M and the crowned D, under IHS); crop x1400-1750 y2000-2350 of the full CH image (4096 x 3275)
url: https://collection.cooperhewitt.org/objects/18616385/
image: https://images.collection.cooperhewitt.org/220797_d42186cdbc6ab946_x.jpg
size: 15 x 15 cells
symmetry: full square symmetry (both mirrors, both diagonals, quarter turn)
colours: # = red cross stitch, . = linen ground. Face palette: red on cream, or cream on the blue window
use: star (compass rose / a large star for the window; a corner rosette for the card)
status: VERIFIED
verified: lattice fitted to the stitches (red channel, pitch 16.7 x 16.9 px); 225/225 cells agree, no mismatches; images/ch-18616385-cross-star-15_ver.jpg
notes: A Greek cross with a solid 3 x 3 centre; each arm runs out through an open lozenge (a diamond ring of six stitches threaded on the arm) and ends in a single point, and four short diagonal rays of two stitches sit between the arms. 65 stitches. On the sampler it is a devotional cross beside IHS, but drawn this way it reads as a compass rose or a sparkling star; the matching cross to its right has a barred lozenge instead (not transcribed: its lower half is stitched one thread out of step and does not read cleanly). For a smaller star see ch-18616385-star-11 on the same sampler.
