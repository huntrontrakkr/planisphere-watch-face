colours: # = red cross stitch, . = ground (the sampler mixes a large star-like cross stitch, about 26 px, with a small one, about 13 px)
use: pointers for later work (village / creature / season), too large for the face at 1x
status: UNVERIFIED (no chart: see notes)
verified: not charted; the pieces were viewed on crops of the full image. Pieces taken from them and charted cell for cell: bk-1980-63-ship-globe (the main-mast top), bk-1980-63-anchor and bk-1980-63-lantern (beside the ship), bk-1980-63-flower-urn (the pot of the carnations), bk-1980-63-bird-on-post (beside the sentry box), bk-1980-63-picket-fence (beside the sentry box)
notes: SHIP: a long hull in solid stitching with a gallery rail of single openings (a checker of holes) along the deck, three masts, two with swallow-tailed pennants and an empty oval top, the main mast with the crossed oval top (charted as bk-1980-63-ship-globe); a dog and a figure in a hood stand on deck; at the stern a stepped ramp under a large square flag. TREE OF LIFE over the couple (a man with a stick and a woman holding a flower): a 2-cell trunk, branches of single diagonal stitches, and stepped foliage clusters with openwork (3 x 3 to 7 x 8 lozenges of stitches with single holes), standing on a base line with a row of single-stitch tufts; framed left and right by vine sprays. CARNATION POTS: in the bottom corners, each a stem rising from the urn with a pair of small leaf sprigs, two leafy side shoots carrying hanging bells (fuchsia-like, solid, 11 x 9), two carnations in profile on long single-stitch stalks, a carnation seen as a fan at the top (26 x 12, with diagonal lines of single holes as the petal divisions) over a 5 x 7 bud; a few cells of the fan's open lines are ambiguous on the photograph. SENTRY BOXES: a round-arched box drawn in a one-stitch outline with a soldier standing inside (shako, arms akimbo, 15 x 30), crowned with a flower and two crossed flags; worked in the small stitch on a skewed patch of cotton, so the walls drift off a straight lattice by up to a stitch.
