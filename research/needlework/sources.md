### Johann Sibmacher, *Schön Neues Modelbuch*, Nuremberg 1597
- Object page: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/357195
- The Met, accession 20.16(1-43). Public Domain (CC0). The first German pattern book etched on copper.
- Charts use symbols (four-point star, vertical hatching, diagonal cross, solid) for colours. Band headings "Mit XXXV Gengen" give the band height in rows (Gänge).
- DP363471–DP363486: text pp. 1–3, then bands of 13–63 rows (lozenge lattices, fleurons, eagles, griffins, vases, vines, eight-pointed stars in a lattice; DP363478 lower band 38 rows; DP363480 lower band 17 rows with blue hand-coloured rosettes; DP363481 lower band 16 rows of stepped crosslets).
- DP363490 (p.17): three bands (20/18/18 rows) of square spot motifs, drawn on a diagonal lattice (skip).
- DP363492 (p.19): lower band, 23 rows, five square spot motifs.
- DP363498 (p.25): a page of narrow bands (5–9 rows) in three columns: the best Sibmacher page for narrow bands. → `sibmacher1597-narrow-band-a`, `sibmacher1597-narrow-band-b`
- DP363499 (p.26): bands ending in points (collar and cuff ends). DP363500 (p.27): "Die Außgeschnittene Arbeit" (cutwork).
