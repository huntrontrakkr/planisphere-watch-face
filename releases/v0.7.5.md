A flick of the wrist, and the battery's candle. Under Animations, a flick of the wrist may now draw the constellations' figures in again, star to star, or spin the sky on and let it settle back to its place over a second, as a planisphere's disc does when it is turned; or do nothing, as before.

Under The card, the battery's candle: a small candle standing in the card's lower corner, burnt down as far as the battery has. Its wax is inked as far as the charge and the rest shows as a ghost, so you see at a glance how much is left; its flame turns red when a fifth or less remains, and stands taller while the watch charges. It can be turned off.
