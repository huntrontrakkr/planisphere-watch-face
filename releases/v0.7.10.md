The constellations spread out from the pole, whatever the weather.

The chart of the constellations now spreads out from the pole in a widening ring with a softly dithered edge. Each figure is drawn out from the middle to the window's edge as the ring passes, and the face comes back the same way, out from the pole.

Under a grey sky, in rain or snow, or in fog, the chart shows its stars all the same, as a printed chart would: the clouds and the weather are left out while it shows.
