A new option under the time's settings: Gild the time. The figures of the time are outlined in the plate's own gilding, as a scribe gilt or rubricated a heading, so they stand clear of the sky and the scenes behind: red ink on the paper planisphere, gold on the Book of Hours, silver on the shagreen, grey on Flamsteed and the lantern slide. The small raised h and m are left as they are.
