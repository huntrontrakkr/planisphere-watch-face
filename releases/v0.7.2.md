The drawing in whole numbers. The watch has no floating-point unit, and nearly half of each minute's drawing was spent in software arithmetic on fractions. The stars, the constellations' lines, the ecliptic and the Milky Way's grid are now placed with an integer trigonometry of our own, to a 256th of a pixel, the same integers in the prototype, so the two agree to the pixel. A frame takes a little over half the work it did at 0.7.0, which is what the battery pays for; every star lands where it did, to within a pixel, and nothing else changes.
