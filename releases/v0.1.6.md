A new option for the Time 2, under The card: the labours' tools in the corners. Each month's tools take the four corners instead of the season's sprigs, drawn after the woodcuts of the 1490s Shepherds' Calendar and a Paris Book of Hours: the scythe and the hay rake in June, the sickle and a sheaf in July, the flail in August, the baker's peel in December. A tool hangs in each top corner and one stands propped in each bottom corner. On the printed plates they are drawn in the plate's ink. It is off unless you turn it on.
