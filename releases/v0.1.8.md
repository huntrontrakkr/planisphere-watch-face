On the Time 2 nearer the equator the window grew past the ring and covered its band. It is now cut by the ring, its rim meeting the band's rule, as the Round 2's window is cut by its own ring.

When the place's name or its state has no room in the band beside MIDNIGHT, both now stand either side of the fleur-de-lis at the window's top instead (NORFOLK ⚜ VIRGINIA), following the window's edge, the two letter-spaced to the same width as old maps space a name. The state's full name is used wherever it fits, then the old newspaper form (VA.), then the postal code (VA).
