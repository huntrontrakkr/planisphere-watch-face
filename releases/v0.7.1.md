Less work for the battery. The face is drawn afresh each minute, and that drawing is where the watch spends its power; it now takes about a fifth less, and a little more than that with a theme behind the face, with every pixel exactly as before: the sky's shading worked out once for each cell of its grid rather than for every pixel, the Milky Way looked up only where the window is, the ring's rim traced along the window's edges alone, the themes' woodcuts decoded in fewer steps, and the phone's messages, which come a few at a time, answered with one drawing instead of one each.
