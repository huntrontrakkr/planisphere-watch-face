The first release of Planisphere: a star chart and an almanac for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2.

The chart: the sky over where you are, turning behind the card's window as a planisphere's does. About a thousand stars, the Milky Way, the constellations' figures, the Moon in its phase, the planets and Jupiter's moons, with the Sun as the hour hand on the ring; the weather from Open-Meteo, and eclipses drawn as they happen.

The almanac: the date, the next sunrise or sunset, and the sky's news (planets meeting, the Moon passing one, a meteor shower's peak). Twelve calendars to keep, from the Christian and Eastern years and ancient Rome to the Hebrew, Chinese, Islamic and Hindu calendars: about ninety days, the great ones in red, each with an emblem drawn after the carved calendar sticks of England and Scandinavia. Some light every window or fires on the hills, some bring fireworks at the top of each evening hour, and some mark the sky itself: Christmas Eve's first star, the magpies' bridge at Qixi, the rabbit in the Mid-Autumn moon. The Labours of the Months, after the shepherds' calendars of the 1490s, as an option.

Ten plates, each after a real chart or instrument, and a settings page that shows the face live as you choose.
