The place's name on the ring is rethought. In the United States, Canada and Australia the state or province now stands beside the city, in the old newspapers' style (Norfolk, Va.; New York, N.Y.; Calif.; Ont.; N.S.W.), dropping to the postal code (VA, NY, CA) when room is short; elsewhere, the country as before.

A name too long for its room is now shortened rather than left out, the way atlases and catalogues abbreviate: generic words first (St., Ft., Mt.), then leading words to their initials (N. York), then the last word cut back a syllable at a time (Norfolk becomes Norf., Philadelphia Philad. or Phil., Switzerland Switz.), then the initials alone. The watch sets the longest form that fits, since the room beside MIDNIGHT changes with your latitude. Places chosen on the settings page get the same treatment, with the state filled in for places in those three countries.
