On the Round 2, one star disc for every place, as a planisphere has: it is fitted to a 40-degree window, the commonest planisphere's. Nearer the equator the ring's edge hides the farthest sky, as a star disc's edge does; nearer a pole than about 63 degrees the card covers the window's top, where the almanac keeps its room. North and south alike, to either pole.

The gallery's sweep now runs from the equator to the north pole and to the south pole.
