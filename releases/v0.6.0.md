Two new plates. The lantern slide: Flamsteed's chart reversed, white on black, as Victorian lecturers threw the heavens on a screen with a magic lantern. And shagreen: the green ray-skin that cased pocket watches, telescopes and sundials in the 1600s and 1700s, with its pale grain, silver mounts round the ring, and the sky in the window as through the telescope it held.

A fix for the place's name and the tide not reaching some watches: the phone now sends the town's name with every update of the position, and sends the tide again from what it has, so a watch that missed them, or was just updated, gets them the next time the face opens.
