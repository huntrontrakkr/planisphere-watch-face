The animations to watch in the settings, and the candle on the dark plates.

The settings page can now play each animation in its preview, on the plate and card you have chosen: the minute written, the figures drawn in, the sky spun, a shooting star, birds at twilight, and a fireworks display. The ones that need a dark sky, or twilight, play at an hour of the preview's own day that has it. The figures are not offered on the plates that never draw them in. The preview also shows the battery's candle when it is chosen.

The battery's candle is now off unless you choose it. Its unburnt wax is drawn as a dotted outline in the card's ink, so the candle can be read on the black and dark plates as well as the light ones.

The 0.7.6 build carried a stale table and was not published; this release replaces it.
