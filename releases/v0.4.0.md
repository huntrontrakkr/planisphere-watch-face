A fourth theme for the card behind the face: the sea. Under The card, choose Behind the face: The sea. Each month a ship as Hollar or Nooms etched them in the 1640s and 50s rides above the window on the tide where you are: down behind the window at low water, up at high, with the sea drawn round it from the hulls to the horizon. The tide comes from the nearest station in the United States, elsewhere from a marine model; inland the ships ride at the mean.
