New animations, now and then, each with its own switch under Animations (and, like the others, left out while the battery runs low):

- Shooting stars: on a clear dark night a meteor crosses the window as the minute turns. Rarely on most nights; one minute in five near a meteor shower's peak, flying out of the shower's radiant.
- The figures drawn in: once an evening, as dusk brings out the stars, the constellations' figures are drawn in star to star.
- Birds at twilight: in the months birds migrate (March to May, September to November), a skein crosses low through the window at dawn or dusk, once a twilight.
- The village's life: windows light and go dark as the village's people come and go, and the hearth smokes on a cool evening.

Settings from earlier versions keep their choices, with the new animations turned on.
