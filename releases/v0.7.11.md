The settings page in the emulators.

The settings page now works in CloudPebble's emulator, and in the SDK's. Those emulators open a face's settings in a web browser, and a browser won't open the page the way a phone does. So in an emulator the face now opens the same page from the web, at huntrontrakkr.github.io/planisphere-watch-face/settings/, with the face's current settings passed along and returned to the emulator when you save. On a phone nothing changes: the page still opens on its own, without the network. Each release publishes its own page.
