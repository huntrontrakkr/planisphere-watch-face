A flick of the wrist, and the battery's candle.

Under Animations, a flick of the wrist may draw the constellations' figures in again, star to star, or spin the sky on and let it settle back to its place over a second, as a planisphere's disc does when it is turned. It is off unless you choose it.

Under The card, the battery's candle: a candle clock in the card's upper corner, banded as the old hour-candles were, burnt down as far as the battery has, with the charge in small figures beneath it. Its flame turns red when a fifth or less is left, and stands taller while the watch charges. It finds a clear place on every plate and on both watches, set off by a little of the card around it where a scene lies behind. It can be turned off.
