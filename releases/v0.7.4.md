The constellations' figures drawn in, star to star, as often as you like. They are drawn in once an evening as dusk brings out the stars; under Animations, "And again, while the stars are out" now offers them again at the top of each hour of the night, each half hour, or each quarter. Each drawing in is six frames of the whole face, so the oftener, the more of the battery; at dusk only is as before.
