The rain and the chimney smoke blown downwind.

The chart is a planisphere, held up to the sky: facing south, east is on the left and west on the right, and the clouds have always drifted that way, downwind. The rain and the snow leaned the other way, against the wind, and the smoke from the house's chimney rose against it too. Both now go with the wind, as the clouds and the autumn leaves do: a westerly leans the rain to the left and carries the smoke that way, an easterly to the right.

And a word on reading the chart, at the head of the settings page's "The sky" and in the README, for those new to a planisphere: face south and the chart matches the sky before you, east on the left; facing north, left and right swap, as with any star chart.
